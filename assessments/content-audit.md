# Content Audit and Correction Log

**Audit status:** In progress  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** lesson alignment, conversation practice, listening evidence, timing, progression rules, and assessment consistency.

## Learning decisions

- Conversation is the primary objective.
- Explanations and corrections can be in Spanish; English practice increases gradually.
- The 90-day sequence is a guide, not a deadline.
- Learners advance when essential skills are demonstrated, not merely because the next day is scheduled.
- No lesson or assessment is approved without actual evidence.
- Reading a transcript aloud is not listening comprehension.
- This plan is not an official CEFR assessment and does not guarantee B1/B2.
