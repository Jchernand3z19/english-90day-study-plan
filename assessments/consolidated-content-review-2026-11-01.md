# Consolidated content review — 90 lessons and Anki phases 2–3

**Review date:** 2026-11-01  
**Scope:** 90 daily lesson files (Days 1–90), phase curriculum maps and checkpoints, plus the 660 Anki phrase/translation pairs in phases 2 and 3.  
**Status:** targeted inconsistencies corrected; final learner pilot and audio-quality listening remain outside what can be certified from static text inspection.

## Summary

The lesson files were reviewed for objective-to-activity alignment, task load, level progression, language/translation consistency, timing, listening instructions, and checkpoint demands. The review found a small number of concrete inconsistencies and several regional-language improvements. These were corrected in the source lessons.

This is a documented editorial review, not a guarantee that every learner will find every activity easy or that the course is already approved. The learner has not completed the corrected Day 1 pilot; all daily lesson statuses remain pending until supported by real evidence.

## Lesson corrections applied on 2026-11-01

| Lesson | Issue found | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Day 19 | Objective said to compare two locations, while the actual tasks teach distance and how to reach a place. | Reworded the objective to match the actual practice. |
| Day 26 | Two questions used formal Spanish address while the surrounding examples use informal address. | Aligned both questions with informal *tú*. |

| Day 42 | Spanish prompts used formal *usted* while the sample conversation and broader lesson use informal *tú*. | Aligned the questions with informal *tú*. |
| Day 52 | Spanish examples mixed *usted* and *tú*, and the self-check demanded more follow-up questions than the main task. | Standardised to *tú* and aligned the self-check to one or two relevant follow-ups. |

## Additional lesson-level observations

- Phase 1 Days 1–7 now use short, modelled sentence frames and supported exchanges; no long introduction is expected before the checkpoint.
- Phase 1 review days are lighter and do not automatically approve a learner or phase.
- Phase 1 Day 29 and Day 30 now use consistent 20–30 second introduction expectations.
- Phase 2 Days 31–36 move from modelled travel/hotel requests toward short role-plays. Days 38–43 build past-event language and chronological storytelling in stages.
- Phase 2 Days 45–50 focus on one problem, request, and solution at a time. Days 52–58 use relevant follow-ups and natural social exchanges rather than a checklist of unrelated questions.
- Phase 2 Day 59 totals 60 minutes and retains the fixed 100-point assessment weights; Day 60 remains a review/decision day, not an automatic pass.
- Phase 3 Days 61–76 progressively introduce opinions, reasons, respectful disagreement, comparison, discussion, idioms, phrasal verbs, register, and rephrasing. Higher speaking demands remain conditional on readiness, not guaranteed by the calendar.
- The prior direct review of Days 77–90 and the Day 86 repair-dialogue correction are recorded in the content audit. Day 89/90 checkpoint and reflection are not automatic proof of mastery.
- No new edits were made to the remaining lesson files where the reviewed instructions and activities were already aligned.

## Anki language review

A separate direct review covered all 660 Phase 2 and Phase 3 English/Spanish pairs. Candidate entries and recommendations are documented in [Anki linguistic review — Phases 2 and 3](../vocabulary/anki-linguistic-review-phases-2-3-2026-11-01.md).

Notable confirmed English-form issues include:
- Position 487: “I’m major in...” is ungrammatical; use “I’m majoring in...” or “My major is...”.
- Position 796: “I bet you.” is incomplete as a standalone target phrase; replace or provide a complement and context.
- Position 850: “Piece of mind” is a misspelling of “Peace of mind” for the intended meaning.

Other candidates concern unnatural or region-specific Spanish, unclear placeholders, register, or expressions that need a contextual example. The ten Phase 1 candidates are separately documented in [the Phase 1 linguistic review](../vocabulary/anki-linguistic-review-phase-1-2026-11-01.md).
