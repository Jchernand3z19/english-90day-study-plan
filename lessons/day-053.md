# Day 053 — Invitaciones y respuestas

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** invite someone to an activity, accept or decline politely, and suggest an alternative when needed.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes
Review due cards from the 1,000 common English phrases deck. Try to recall each phrase aloud before revealing the answer. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts; do not estimate progress.

## 2. Invitations and responses — 15 minutes

| English | Español | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Would you like to…?** | ¿Te gustaría…? | Invite someone |
| **Do you want to join me?** | ¿Quieres acompañarme? | Invite someone |
| **I'd love to.** | Me encantaría. | Accept warmly |
| **That sounds great.** | Suena muy bien. | Accept positively |
| **I'm sorry, I can't make it.** | Lo siento, no puedo ir. | Decline politely |
| **Maybe another time?** | ¿Quizá en otra ocasión? | Keep the connection |
| **How about Saturday instead?** | ¿Qué tal el sábado en su lugar? | Suggest an alternative |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall its meaning. Then make one invitation, one acceptance, and one polite decline using your own ideas or clearly labelled role-play.


## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the conversation twice without showing you the script first.

> Ava: Would you like to join us for dinner on Saturday?
>
> Ben: Thanks for inviting me. What time?
>
> Ava: Around seven.
>
> Ben: That sounds great. Should I bring anything?
>
> Ava: Just yourself. See you then!
>
> Ben: Great. Thanks for the invitation.

**Questions**
1. What is Ben invited to do?
2. Which day is the invitation for?
3. What time will they meet?
4. Does Ava ask Ben to bring anything?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes
**Main task:** Invitaciones y respuestas. Speak or role-play for 1–3 minutes, using keywords rather than a complete written script.

Then answer aloud:
1. What would you say first?
2. What important detail or reason should you include?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat once with less support. If using an AI tutor, request one prompt at a time and corrections in Spanish after your response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes
Without looking at notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain what your examples mean.
- Ask one follow-up or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. Speaking-focused tasks require an actual oral attempt. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.

Use **In progress** if started, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when the work and evidence are complete.
