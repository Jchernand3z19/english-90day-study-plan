# Day 090 — Final reflection and next plan

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** review real evidence from the 90-day plan, recognise progress honestly, and choose practical priorities for the next 30 days.

Day 90 is a reflection and planning day, not a second final exam. You do not need to claim a particular CEFR level. Compare what you can demonstrate now with your Day 1 evidence.

## 1. Retrieve useful language — 10 minutes

Without looking at your notes, say ten English phrases you can use in real conversations. Include:
- two ways to ask for clarification;
- two phrases for opinions or reasons;
- two phrases for describing past events;
- two phrases for everyday requests;
- two phrases you personally find useful.

Then check your notes and record which phrases you recalled without help. Do not count a phrase as independent recall if you had to read it.

## 2. Review your evidence — 15 minutes

Look at your tracker and daily logs. Answer honestly:
1. How many lessons have actual evidence and an assessment result?
2. Which tasks did you complete without reading a full script?
3. Which listening activities did you actually hear, rather than only read?
4. What corrections appeared repeatedly?
5. What evidence shows progress compared with Day 1?

Do not change a lesson to Approved just to complete the plan. Keep unassessed or incomplete lessons in their honest status.

## 3. Short speaking reflection — 10 minutes

Speak for 1–2 minutes in English. Use these prompts:
- **At the beginning, I could…**
- **Now I can…**
- **I still find … difficult because…**
- **One thing I learned is…**
- **Next month, I want to…**

You may prepare keywords in Spanish, but try to deliver the reflection in English. If you cannot yet say a sentence, write down the idea and practise it with a tutor; this is useful evidence, not a failure.

## 4. Identify your next priorities — 15 minutes

Rate each skill based on evidence, not feelings alone:

| Skill | Evidence to review | Priority (High/Medium/Low) |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Correct answers to heard dialogues | Not rated |
| Speaking | Recorded or observed oral attempts | Not rated |
| Vocabulary | Phrases recalled without notes | Not rated |
| Grammar | Recurring corrections in your own sentences | Not rated |
| Interaction | Follow-up questions and clarification attempts | Not rated |

Choose the two weakest skills as your first priorities. If the evidence is missing, mark **Not assessed** instead of guessing.

## 5. Create a realistic 30-day plan — 10 minutes

Write three specific goals for the next month. Each goal should name an action and a way to check it. Examples:
- Listen to and answer questions about three short dialogues per week.
- Record a two-minute spoken response twice per week and review recurring corrections.
- Practise ten useful phrases in original sentences and revisit them during later sessions. The existing deck will be reviewed and organized when the user shares the file; this lesson does not require Anki.

Choose a schedule you can maintain. Do not add calendar automation yet; the learning content and routine should be validated first.

## Completion and interpretation

Day 90 is complete when the reflection, evidence review, and next plan are recorded. There is no pass score for reflection alone. The 90-day plan does not guarantee B1/B2 and does not certify a CEFR level. For a formal level estimate, use an appropriate standardised assessment.

Record the date, actual time spent, evidence reviewed, priorities, and next-30-day goals in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, and **Approved** only when the reflection and plan are complete.
