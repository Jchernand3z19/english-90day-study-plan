# Day 085 — Answering unexpected questions

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** respond to an unexpected question without freezing, ask for clarification when needed, and organise a short answer.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use each in a new sentence. Record actual counts.

## 2. Buy time and organise your answer — 15 minutes

| English | Meaning in Spanish | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **That's a good question.** | Es una buena pregunta. | Gives you a moment to think. |
| **Let me think for a moment.** | Déjeme pensar un momento. | Buys a little time. |
| **Could you clarify what you mean by…?** | ¿Podría aclarar qué quiere decir con…? | Checks the question. |
| **If I understand correctly, you're asking…** | Si entiendo bien, está preguntando… | Confirms your interpretation. |
| **One example that comes to mind is…** | Un ejemplo que se me ocurre es… | Starts a specific answer. |
| **I don't know the exact answer, but…** | No sé la respuesta exacta, pero… | Responds honestly when unsure. |
| **To put it another way…** | Dicho de otra manera… | Explains an idea more clearly. |

Use this simple structure: **pause → clarify if needed → answer directly → add one reason or example**. It is acceptable to say you do not know. Do not invent facts to fill silence.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the exchange twice without showing you the script first.

> Interviewer: What's a skill you'd like to learn, and why?  
> Candidate: That's a good question. I'd like to improve my English because it would help me communicate with more people.  
> Interviewer: How would you practise?  
> Candidate: I would listen to short conversations and practise speaking every day.  
> Interviewer: What would you do if you didn't understand someone?  
> Candidate: I'd ask them to repeat or explain the question. I think it's better to check than to guess.

Questions:
1. What skill does the candidate want to improve?
2. Why?
3. How would the candidate practise?
4. What would the candidate do when they did not understand someone?

Check after answering, then shadow two lines.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Answer each question aloud for 30–45 seconds. Use keywords, not a prepared paragraph.

1. What is something new you would like to learn?
2. What would you do if you had a free day tomorrow?
3. Describe a small problem you solved.
4. What is one thing you have changed your mind about?
5. What would you do if you did not understand a question?

Ask a tutor or AI partner to present questions one at a time in a random order. Request feedback in Spanish after your answer. Retry the answer that was hardest, using the response structure above.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Answer at least three unexpected questions aloud.
- Use a phrase to buy time or clarify.
- Give one reason or example.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable responses. If you answer only “yes/no” or freeze without trying a repair phrase, mark **Review**.
