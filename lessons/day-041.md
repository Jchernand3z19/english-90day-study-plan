# Day 041 — Viajes y experiencias pasadas

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** describe a past trip, including destination, activities, and one memorable detail.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult cards and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |\n|---|---|\n| **I went to… last year.** | Fui a… el año pasado. |\n| **I stayed for… days.** | Me quedé durante… días. |\n| **We visited…** | Visitamos… |\n| **The best part was…** | La mejor parte fue… |\n| **It was crowded / quiet / beautiful.** | Estaba lleno / tranquilo / era hermoso. |\n| **I'd like to go back because…** | Me gustaría volver porque… |

Say each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish meaning and try to retrieve it. Make three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Nina: Have you ever visited the coast?\n> Omar: Yes, I went last year and stayed for three days.\n> Nina: What was the best part?\n> Omar: We visited a small beach. It was quiet in the morning and very beautiful.\n> Nina: Would you like to go back?\n> Omar: Yes, because I want to explore the nearby towns too.

**Questions**
1. When did Omar visit the coast?\n2. How long did he stay?\n3. What was the best part?\n4. Why would he like to return?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Describe a real trip or clearly labelled imagined trip for 60 seconds. Mention where you went, how long you stayed, two activities, and one thing you liked. Answer one follow-up question about the experience.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use past-tense verbs to describe a completed trip.\n- Include at least two details and a reason.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable oral production. If the core task still requires reading a full script, mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
