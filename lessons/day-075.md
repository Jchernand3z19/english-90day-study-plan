# Day 075 — Short professional introduction

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** introduce yourself professionally in 45–60 seconds, describe relevant experience truthfully, and ask the other person a question.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Build a short introduction — 15 minutes

| English phrase | Meaning in Spanish |
|---|---|
| **Hello, it's nice to meet you.** | Hola, mucho gusto. |
| **I work in… / My background is in…** | Trabajo en… / Mi experiencia es en… |
| **I have experience with…** | Tengo experiencia con… |
| **One thing I enjoy is…** | Algo que disfruto es… |
| **I'm currently improving my skills in…** | Actualmente estoy mejorando mis habilidades en… |
| **What kind of work do you do?** | ¿A qué tipo de trabajo se dedica? |
| **It was nice speaking with you.** | Fue un gusto conversar con usted. |

**Structure:** greeting + current role or real background + one relevant strength or interest + a question. Keep it concise. Do not claim qualifications or achievements you cannot support.

Write keywords only:
- current work or background;
- one real task or skill;
- one professional interest or learning goal;
- one question for the other person.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this exchange twice without showing the script first.

> Nina: Hello, I'm Nina. It's nice to meet you.  
> Omar: Nice to meet you too. I'm Omar, and I work in business reporting.  
> Nina: What kind of work do you do?  
> Omar: I prepare reports and help teams understand their results. I'm also improving my presentation skills.  
> Nina: That sounds useful. What kind of projects do you enjoy?  
> Omar: I enjoy projects where I can organise information and explain it clearly.

Answer aloud:
1. What area does Omar work in?
2. What does he do?
3. Which skill is he improving?
4. What kind of projects does he enjoy?

Check after answering and shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the exchange, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading the script is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Give your own 45–60 second professional introduction. Use your real background and simple English. You may use a fictional role-play identity only if you clearly label it as fictional.

Then answer two follow-up questions:
- **What kind of work do you enjoy?**
- **What skill are you working to improve?**

Finish by asking the other person one question. If using an AI tutor, request feedback on clarity and natural wording in Spanish, then repeat the introduction without reading.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Introduce yourself for 45–60 seconds.
- Include one truthful example of experience or a skill.
- Ask one relevant question.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an oral attempt and an understandable introduction. If you rely on a full script, mark **Review** and retry using only keywords.
