# Day 088 — Week 12 review

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 25–35 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** review opinions, professional conversation, listening, and clarification before the final integrated assessment.

This is a lighter session. Do not add new vocabulary unless it is needed to correct a recurring gap.

## 1. Retrieval — 5 minutes

Without notes, say:
- one phrase to give an opinion;
- one connector to give a reason or example;
- one phrase to ask a follow-up question;
- one professional introduction phrase;
- one phrase to clarify a misunderstanding.

Then check your notes and mark which you recalled independently.

## 2. Short listening — 10 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing it first.

> Carla: I think practising a little every day is more effective than studying for hours once a week.  
> Ben: I agree that regular practice helps, but some people need longer sessions for complex topics.  
> Carla: That's a fair point. Maybe the best schedule depends on the learner and the goal.  
> Ben: Yes. The important thing is to review what you learn and use it in conversation.

Questions:
1. What does Carla prefer?
2. What concern does Ben mention?
3. What does Carla say the schedule depends on?
4. What does Ben say is important?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 3. Speaking review — 10–15 minutes

Speak for 60–90 seconds about this question: **“What helps someone learn a new skill effectively?”** Give your opinion, two reasons, and one example. Then respond respectfully to this alternative view: **“People learn best when they practise for a long time in one session.”**

Use keywords, not a complete script. Ask for feedback in Spanish and retry one corrected sentence.

## 4. Final readiness check — 5 minutes

- Can you state an opinion and support it?
- Can you respond to a different viewpoint?
- Can you introduce yourself and answer a follow-up?
- Can you ask for clarification instead of pretending to understand?
- Did you actually complete the listening task?

Record the two skills that need the most attention. Day 89 is not automatically passed by finishing this review.
