# Day 017 — Transport, tickets, and schedules

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** Ask about a route, departure time, and ticket.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Practice these expressions. Read the English first, explain the meaning in Spanish, then say the English phrase without looking.

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| What time does the bus leave? | ¿A qué hora sale el autobús? |
| Where can I buy a ticket? | ¿Dónde puedo comprar un billete/boleto? |
| Is this seat taken? | ¿Está ocupado este asiento? |
| How long does it take? | ¿Cuánto tiempo tarda? |

**Practice tip:** learn each expression as a complete chunk. Focus on meaning and use before trying to memorize grammar terminology. Use your own real information; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Passenger: Excuse me, does this bus go to the city centre?
>
> Driver: Yes, it does. You need a ticket before you get on.
>
> Passenger: How much is a ticket?
>
> Driver: Three dollars. The bus leaves in ten minutes.
>
> Passenger: Where can I buy the ticket?
>
> Driver: At the machine next to the bus stop.

**Questions**
1. Where does the passenger want to go?
2. How much is a ticket?
3. When does the bus leave?
4. Where can the passenger buy a ticket?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production practice — 15 minutes

**Core exchange: find the right bus.** You are at a bus station and want to go to the city centre. Start with one question: “Does this bus go to the city centre?” The clerk answers: “Yes, bus 4 goes there.” Respond naturally with “Thank you” or “Great, thank you.”

**Add one useful follow-up:** ask “What time does it leave?” The clerk answers: “In 15 minutes.” Repeat the key detail in your own words if you can: “Okay, in 15 minutes. Thanks.”

**Optional extension — buying the ticket:** ask where to buy it and how much it costs. The clerk can answer: “At the machine” and “Three dollars.” Do this only if the first exchange feels comfortable. Switching roles is optional, not required.

If you do not understand an answer, use “Could you repeat that, please?” or “Could you speak more slowly, please?” Practise the core exchange once with the model, then once using only keywords. The aim is to complete a small, natural interaction, not to ask every possible question.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Produce at least three of today's target phrases in English.
- Use two phrases in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of the phrases you used.
- Ask for clarification in English if you get stuck.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the self-check items are completed correctly and the main speaking task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, an attempted oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
