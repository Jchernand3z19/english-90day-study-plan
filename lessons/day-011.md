# Day 011 — Special requests at a restaurant

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** make a polite request about food and ask for a simple change to an order.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Could I have the menu, please? | ¿Me trae el menú, por favor? |
| Could I have this without onions? | ¿Podría prepararlo sin cebolla? |
| Is this spicy? | ¿Esto es picante? |
| Just water, please. | Solo agua, por favor. |
| Could we have the bill, please? | ¿Nos trae la cuenta, por favor? |
| I'll be right back. | Vuelvo enseguida. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and try to retrieve the meaning. Choose three phrases to use in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Customer: Excuse me, could I have the menu, please?  
Server: Certainly. Here you are.  
Customer: Thank you. Could I have the soup without onions?  
Server: Of course. Would you like anything to drink?  
Customer: Just water, please.  
Server: No problem. I'll be right back.

**Questions**
1. What does the customer ask for first?  
2. What change does the customer request?  
3. What drink does the customer order?  
4. What does the server say at the end?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Role-play ordering a meal with one special request and a drink. Use “Could I have…?” rather than a direct command.

Ask for one or two corrections in Spanish if a tutor is available, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
