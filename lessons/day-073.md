# Day 073 — Dialogue with natural expressions

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use common conversational expressions to react, clarify, and keep a dialogue moving naturally.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |\n|---|---|\n| **No way!** | ¡No puede ser! |\n| **That makes sense.** | Eso tiene sentido. |\n| **Fair enough.** | Está bien / es razonable. |\n| **Actually,…** | En realidad… |\n| **By the way,…** | Por cierto… |\n| **I get what you mean.** | Entiendo lo que quieres decir. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples and explain their meaning in Spanish.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Rob: I started walking to work because parking is expensive.\n> May: No way! How long does it take?\n> Rob: About twenty minutes. Actually, I enjoy the walk now.\n> May: That makes sense. You get some exercise too.\n> Rob: Exactly. By the way, do you want to walk together tomorrow?\n> May: Fair enough. Let's meet at eight.

**Questions**
1. Why did Rob start walking to work?\n2. How long does the walk take?\n3. What does Rob enjoy now?\n4. What plan do they make?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play a conversation about a change in your routine, a surprising event, or a new activity. Use three natural expressions, but only where they fit. Ask a follow-up question and respond to the other person's reaction.

Use keywords, not a complete script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use three expressions appropriately.\n- Ask and answer a follow-up question.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. If you still need a full script, mark **Review** and repeat the task with fewer notes.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
