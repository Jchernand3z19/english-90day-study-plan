# Day 083 — Advantages and disadvantages

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** compare two options fairly by describing a benefit, a drawback, and the conditions that affect the choice.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **One advantage is…** | Una ventaja es… |
| **A possible drawback is…** | Una posible desventaja es… |
| **Compared with…** | Comparado con… |
| **It depends on…** | Depende de… |
| **On balance,…** | En general / sopesándolo… |
| **The best option may be…** | La mejor opción podría ser… |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Eva: Do you prefer working at home or in an office?
> Noah: One advantage of working at home is that I save travel time.
> Eva: What's a possible drawback?
> Noah: It can be harder to ask a quick question or feel connected to the team.
> Eva: So which is better?
> Noah: It depends on the job. On balance, a mix of home and office days may work best for some teams.

**Questions**
1. What advantage does Noah mention?
2. What drawback does he describe?
3. What does the choice depend on?
4. What option does Noah suggest may work well?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Compare two familiar choices, such as learning alone or in a group, cooking at home or eating out, or travelling by bus or taxi. Give one advantage and one drawback for each, then make a balanced conclusion.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Describe a benefit and a drawback.
- Use **it depends on** or **on balance** appropriately.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion
