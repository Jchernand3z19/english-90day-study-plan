# Day 030 — Corrección de errores y cierre

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** practice **corrección de errores y cierre** in understandable, useful English.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from the 1,000 common English phrases deck. Try to say the phrase before revealing the answer. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use each in a new sentence. Record actual counts; do not estimate progress.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Practice these flexible expressions and adapt them to today's topic:

| English | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Could you help me, please? | Use it to ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| Could you repeat that, please? | Use it to ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| I'd like… | Use it to ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| How do I get to…? | Use it to ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| I usually… | Use it to ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| Let me think for a moment. | Gain a little time to organize an answer. |
| What I mean is… | Rephrase an idea more clearly. |

Write three topic-specific examples. Explain their meaning in Spanish, then say each English sentence without looking. Use truthful personal information; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

1. Listen to a short, level-appropriate audio related to today's topic if a suitable source is available.
2. Identify the main idea and two useful expressions.
3. Repeat a short segment, imitating rhythm and word stress.
4. Say the expressions again without reading.

If no audio source was used, record that honestly. Reading aloud is useful but is not the same as listening practice.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes

**Main task:** Corrección de errores y cierre. Explain or perform the situation aloud for 1–3 minutes, depending on difficulty. Use keywords rather than reading a complete script.

Answer these prompts:
1. What would you say first in this situation?
2. What detail or reason should you include?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat once with less support. An AI tutor can present one prompt at a time and give corrections in Spanish after you answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at your notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two phrases in original sentences or a dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of your examples.
- Ask one follow-up question or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, attempting the oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
