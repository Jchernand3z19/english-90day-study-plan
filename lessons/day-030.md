# Day 030 — Corrección de errores y cierre de fase 1

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use the actual Day 29 assessment results to correct two weak skills and decide whether the foundations are secure enough to continue.

This is not an automatic pass. If Day 29 is incomplete or not assessed, complete the missing tasks first or record what remains unassessed.

## 1. Review the evidence — 5 minutes

Open your Day 29 results. Identify the two lowest-scoring skills from personal introduction, everyday role-play, directions/routines, listening, or phrase retrieval. If no score was recorded, mark the skills **Not assessed** rather than guessing.

## 2. Correct real errors — 10 minutes

Choose up to three errors from your actual Day 29 answers. For each one, record:
- what you said;
- the corrected English phrase;
- a brief explanation in Spanish;
- a new sentence using the corrected pattern.

Do not invent errors just to fill the list. If there are no recorded errors, ask a tutor to give you three beginner prompts and record the feedback you actually receive.

## 3. Targeted listening — 10 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Customer: Excuse me, how do I get to the supermarket?
>
> Local: Go straight for one block and turn right. It's next to the pharmacy.
>
> Customer: Is it far from here?
>
> Local: No, it's about a five-minute walk.
>
> Customer: Thank you. Could you repeat the last part, please?
>
> Local: It's about a five-minute walk.

**Questions**
1. Where does the customer want to go?
2. Which direction should the customer turn?
3. What is next to the supermarket?
4. How long is the walk?

Answer before checking the script. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Retry your weakest speaking skill — 15–20 minutes

Repeat the relevant Day 29 task with less help:
- **Introduction:** speak for 45–60 seconds using keywords.
- **Everyday role-play:** ask two questions and make one polite request.
- **Directions and routine:** give a simple route and describe three daily activities.
- **Phrase retrieval:** say five useful phrases without looking and use one clarification phrase.

If you struggle, simplify the prompt, correct one or two errors, and try again. Do not read a complete script.

## 5. Decide the next step — 5 minutes

- **Ready to progress:** the required Day 29 tasks are assessed, the total is at least 80/100, and your main message is understandable.
- **Review:** the score is below 80/100 or a core skill remains unreliable. Repeat the weak skill before moving to harder content.
- **Incomplete:** one or more required tasks were not performed or could not be assessed.

Use the fixed scoring in the phase-assessments document. Record one strength and one next target. Do not mark Phase 1 passed simply because Day 30 is complete.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Day 29 result, errors corrected, listening method and answers, retry task, decision, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **Review** if more practice is needed and **Approved** only when the required evidence meets the criteria.
