## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Mia: Where is the pharmacy?
>
> Leo: It's next to the supermarket, across from the bank.
>
> Mia: Is it near the bus stop?
>
> Leo: Yes. The bus stop is in front of the supermarket.
>
> Mia: So the pharmacy is beside the supermarket and across from the bank?
>
> Leo: That's right.

**Questions**
1. What place is Mia looking for?
2. What is next to the pharmacy?
3. What is across from the pharmacy?
4. Where is the bus stop?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production practice — 15 minutes

Complete the task aloud: **Describe where five places are on an imaginary map.**

Then answer these prompts without reading a full script:
1. What is the main thing you need to say in this situation?
2. Which phrase from today's list helps you say it?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat the task once using only keywords. If working with an AI tutor, ask it to present one prompt at a time and give corrections in Spanish after you answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Produce at least three of today's target phrases in English.
- Use two phrases in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of the phrases you used.
- Ask for clarification in English if you get stuck.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the self-check items are completed correctly and the main speaking task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, an attempted oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
