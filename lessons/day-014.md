## 1. Quick retrieval — 5 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say the phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Recall everyday requests — 10 minutes

Without notes, say a phrase for each purpose:

| Situation | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Ask a price | How much is this? | ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? |
| Ask for a size | Do you have this in medium? | ¿Lo tiene en talla mediana? |
| Order food | I'd like a sandwich, please. | Quisiera un sándwich, por favor. |
| Ask for a menu | Could I have the menu, please? | ¿Me trae el menú, por favor? |
| Request a change | Could I have this without onions? | ¿Podría prepararlo sin cebolla? |

Choose three phrases and adapt them to your own role-play. Say each one twice without reading.

## 3. Mini role-play — 5–10 minutes

Choose shopping or ordering at a restaurant. Ask two questions and make one polite request. If a tutor or AI is available, request one correction in Spanish and repeat the corrected phrase.

## 4. Choose one target — 2 minutes

Record one phrase you remembered easily and one that needs more practice. No score is required today.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki cards reviewed, phrases practised, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; a full rest day remains pending and does not count as completed practice.
