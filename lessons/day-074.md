# Day 074 — Week 10 review

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 25–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** review natural expressions, professional conversation, and rephrasing before continuing to more complex discussions.

This is a light review day. A full rest day is allowed; if you rest, leave this lesson pending.

## 1. Retrieval — 5 minutes

Without notes, say one phrase for each purpose:
- give an opinion;
- explain an idea more clearly;
- react naturally to another person;
- ask a follow-up question;
- gain time before answering.

Check your notes and mark which phrases you recalled independently.

## 2. Mini listening — 8–10 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this exchange twice without showing the script first.

> Mia: I tried a new class after work yesterday.
>
> Ben: Really? What was it like?
>
> Mia: It was challenging, but I learned a lot. At first I was nervous, but the teacher explained everything clearly.
>
> Ben: That makes sense. Would you do it again?
>
> Mia: Yes. I think practising regularly will help me improve.

Questions:
1. What did Mia try?
2. How did she feel at first?
3. What helped her?
4. Would she do it again?

If you did not actually hear the exchange, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 3. Speaking review — 10 minutes

Speak for 60 seconds about a new activity, a skill you are learning, or a recent experience. Include one detail, one opinion, and one reason. Then answer a follow-up question without reading a full script.

If a tutor is available, ask for one correction in Spanish and repeat the corrected sentence.

## 4. Choose one target — 2–5 minutes

Write down one expression you used successfully and one skill to review next. Do not add new vocabulary unless it fixes a real gap.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled, listening method, speaking attempt, and next review target. This review does not automatically approve the week. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started and **Approved** only when the required work is complete.
