# Day 006 — Combine an introduction and small talk

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** combine a greeting, introduction, one personal detail, a question, and a polite closing.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it. Record only the number you actually reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| I work in… | Trabajo en… |
| I like… | Me gusta… |
| What do you do? | ¿A qué te dedicas? |
| What do you like doing? | ¿Qué te gusta hacer? |
| That sounds interesting. | Eso suena interesante. |
| It was nice meeting you. | Fue un gusto conocerte/conocerle. |

Read each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish column and try to recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and say them without looking.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. My name is Juan.  
A: Nice to meet you, Juan. I'm Alex. Where are you from?  
B: I'm from Honduras. What do you do?  
A: I work in data analysis. What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music.  
A: It was nice meeting you.  
B: Nice meeting you too. Have a good day!

**Questions**
1. Where is Juan from?  
2. What does Alex do?  
3. What does Juan like doing?  
4. How do they end the conversation?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then repeat two lines, imitating rhythm and pronunciation. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Give a 30–45 second introduction with one real detail and one interest. Ask a question and close politely. Use keywords, not a full script.

If a tutor or AI tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish. Repeat the corrected sentence. Try the task again with less help.
