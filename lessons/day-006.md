# Day 006 — First-Meeting Conversation

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** combine greetings, personal questions, spelling or clarification, and small talk in one conversation.

## 1. Anki warm-up — 10 minutes

Review phrases from Days 1–5. Choose any phrase you could not recall yesterday and try again before looking.

## 2. Conversation building blocks — 15 minutes

Use this order as a guide, not as a script you must memorize:

1. Greeting: “Hi! How are you?”
2. Introduction: “My name is… Nice to meet you.”
3. Background: “Where are you from?”
4. Work or interests: “What do you do?” / “What do you like to do in your free time?”
5. Follow-up: “Really? Tell me more.”
6. Closing: “It was nice meeting you. Have a good day!”

Useful clarification phrases:
- “Could you repeat that, please?”
- “Could you speak more slowly, please?”
- “Let me think for a moment.”

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Read this complete dialogue twice, then repeat it with the roles reversed.

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. How about you?  
A: I'm doing well. My name is Alex.  
B: Nice to meet you, Alex. I'm Juan.  
A: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?  
B: I'm from Honduras. What do you do?  
A: I work in data analysis. How about you?  
B: I work with reports and dashboards.  
A: That sounds interesting. What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music.  
A: It was nice meeting you.  
B: Nice meeting you too. Have a good day!

## 4. Speak — 15 minutes

Complete three rounds:

- **Round 1:** read the dialogue aloud.
- **Round 2:** use only keywords, not the full sentences.
- **Round 3:** change two questions or answers and continue naturally.

If practicing with an AI tutor, ask it to play the other person, ask one question at a time, and wait for your answer. Request corrections in Spanish after each response or at the end.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Try to complete a first-meeting conversation that includes:

- A greeting and a response.
- Your name and where you are from.
- One question about the other person.
- One follow-up question.
- A polite goodbye.

**Suggested pass:** complete the exchange understandably with no more than occasional reference to keywords. If you need to read every line, mark it for review and repeat later.

## Evidence

Record the approximate conversation length, which parts you could say without reading, your three most difficult phrases, and the result of the self-check.
