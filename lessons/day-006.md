## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the complete dialogue twice without showing it first. Answer the questions, then check the text and repeat it with the roles reversed.

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. How about you?  
A: I'm doing well. My name is Alex.  
B: Nice to meet you, Alex. I'm Juan.  
A: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?  
B: I'm from Honduras. What do you do?  
A: I work in data analysis. How about you?  
B: I work with reports and dashboards.  
A: That sounds interesting. What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music.  
A: It was nice meeting you.  
B: Nice meeting you too. Have a good day!

**Questions**
1. Where is Juan from?
2. What kind of work does Alex do?
3. What does Juan like doing in his free time?
4. How do they end the conversation?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.
