# Day 006 — Put a short introduction together

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 45–55 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** combine a greeting, name, country, and one interest in a short supported exchange.

## 1. Recall — 5–8 minutes

Review these four chunks: “Hi”, “My name is…”, “I'm from…”, “I like…”. Use the lesson notes.

## 2. Review the sentence frames — 10 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Hi. | Hola. |
| My name is… | Me llamo… |
| I'm from Honduras. | Soy de Honduras. |
| I like music. | Me gusta la música. |
| Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto. |

Repeat each sentence after hearing it. Change only the personal information in the blanks.

## 3. Guided listening — 10–12 minutes

Listen twice without reading:

> A: Hi. My name is Alex.  
> B: Hello, Alex. I'm Sam.  
> A: Nice to meet you.  
> B: Nice to meet you too.  
> A: I'm from Canada.  
> B: I'm from Honduras.

Answer:
1. What is A's name?
2. What is B's name?
3. Where is B from?

If audio was not played, mark listening **Not assessed**. Reveal the script only after listening.

## 4. Supported conversation — 15–18 minutes

Practise this exchange with the model visible. Fill only the blanks with your own information:

- A: Hi. My name is ______.
- B: Hello. I'm ______.
- A: Nice to meet you.
- B: Nice to meet you too.
- A: I'm from ______.
- B: I'm from ______.

If comfortable, add one sentence: “I like music.” Do not add a job description or a long explanation.

## 5. Check and evidence — 5 minutes

Record whether you could say your name and country with the model, whether you added the interest sentence, and whether you actually listened to audio. Mark *Ready for review* or *Review*.

**Completion rule:** a short, supported exchange is the target. A 30–45 second introduction is not required at this stage.
