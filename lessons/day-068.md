# Day 068 — Common idioms in context

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** understand and use a small set of common idioms in natural, everyday conversation.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Idioms in context — 15 minutes

Learn these expressions as complete chunks. They are informal and are most natural in everyday conversation.

| Idiom | Meaning in Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **It's up to you.** | Tú decides / depende de ti. | We can eat here or at home. It's up to you. |
| **That sounds familiar.** | Eso me suena. | Her name sounds familiar, but I can't remember where we met. |
| **I'm just kidding.** | Solo estoy bromeando. | Don't worry—I'm just kidding. |
| **No worries.** | No te preocupes / no hay problema. | Sorry I'm late. — No worries. |
| **A piece of cake.** | Muy fácil; literalmente, “un pedazo de pastel”. | The exercise was a piece of cake. |
| **On the same page.** | De acuerdo / entender lo mismo sobre un tema. | Let's review the plan so we're on the same page. |

**Practice without overload**
1. First, learn three core expressions: **It's up to you**, **No worries**, and **I'm just kidding**. Match each to its Spanish meaning and make one original example aloud.
2. Then read the other three expressions (**That sounds familiar**, **A piece of cake**, and **on the same page**) to recognise their meaning in context. You do not need to memorise all six today.
3. Do not force an idiom into a formal or serious situation if it sounds unnatural.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below **without showing you the text first**. Listen twice, then answer the questions aloud. If you are studying alone, use a read-aloud feature and cover the script while listening.

**Listening script — reveal only after the first two listens**

> Maya: Do you want to choose the restaurant tonight?  
> Leo: It's up to you. I'm happy with anything.  
> Maya: There's a new place near the station.  
> Leo: That sounds familiar. Is it the place Sam told us about?  
> Maya: Yes, that's the one. The menu looks easy to understand.  
> Leo: Great. Choosing dinner should be a piece of cake, then.  
> Maya: I'm just kidding, but I hope we don't spend an hour deciding!  
> Leo: No worries. Let's check the menu and make a plan.

**Comprehension questions**
1. Who gets to choose the restaurant?
2. Why does the restaurant sound familiar to Leo?
3. What does Leo mean by “a piece of cake”?
4. Name two idioms from the dialogue.

Check your answers against the script after attempting them. Then shadow two lines, copying the rhythm and stress.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Complete both tasks aloud, using keywords rather than a full written script.

**Task A — Choose a plan (2 minutes):** Role-play with a friend or AI tutor. Decide where to eat, what to do at the weekend, or which film to watch. Use **It's up to you**, **No worries**, and one other idiom naturally.

**Task B — Explain an idiom (1 minute):** Explain the meaning of two idioms in simple English, then give an example. You may use Spanish to check the meaning, but produce the examples in English.

If using an AI tutor, ask for one prompt at a time and corrections in Spanish after you answer. Retry one corrected sentence without reading.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Explain at least four idioms in Spanish.
- Use three idioms correctly in original sentences.
- Answer at least three of the four listening questions correctly.
- Complete an oral attempt.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 using the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable spoken examples. If not, mark **Review** and practise the three weakest expressions before trying again.
