# Day 027 — Describe a typical day

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** connect routine vocabulary into a short, understandable description of a typical day.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| I get up at… | Me levanto a las… |
| After that,… | Después de eso,… |
| I have lunch at noon. | Almuerzo al mediodía. |
| I finish work at… | Termino de trabajar a las… |
| In the evening,… | Por la noche,… |
| I go to bed at… | Me acuesto a las… |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and use them in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Alex: Can you describe your typical day?  
Bea: I get up at seven and have breakfast.  
Alex: What do you do after breakfast?  
Bea: I start work at eight. I have lunch at noon and finish work at five.  
Alex: What do you do in the evening?  
Bea: I cook dinner, study English for a while, and go to bed at ten.

**Questions**
1. What time does Bea get up?  
2. When does she have lunch?  
3. What time does she finish work?  
4. Name two things she does in the evening.

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Speak for 45–60 seconds about a typical day. Use sequence words such as first, after that, and in the evening. Do not read a complete script.

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
