# Day 064 — Comparing options and consequences

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** compare two options, explain advantages and disadvantages, and describe likely consequences.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Compare and explain — 15 minutes

| English | Meaning in Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Compared with…** | Comparado con… | Compared with driving, taking the bus is cheaper. |
| **On the one hand… / On the other hand…** | Por un lado… / Por otro lado… | On the one hand, it is fast. On the other hand, it is expensive. |
| **The main advantage is…** | La principal ventaja es… | The main advantage is that I can practise every day. |
| **The main drawback is…** | La principal desventaja es… | The main drawback is the cost. |
| **If I choose…, I will…** | Si elijo…, voy a… | If I choose the bus, I will save money. |
| **It depends on…** | Depende de… | It depends on the time and price. |

**Practice:** Compare learning alone with learning in a group. Say one advantage and one drawback for each option. Use at least three expressions from the table.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Carla: Should we take the bus or a taxi to the airport?  
> Luis: The bus is cheaper, but it takes longer.  
> Carla: That's true. The main advantage of a taxi is that it's faster.  
> Luis: On the other hand, traffic could make it slow too.  
> Carla: It depends on our budget and the time we have.  
> Luis: If we take the bus, we'll save money. If we're late, a taxi might be better.

Answer aloud:
1. Which option is cheaper?
2. What is the main advantage of a taxi?
3. What two things does Carla say the decision depends on?
4. What consequence does Luis mention if they take the bus?

Check the script after answering, then shadow two lines.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Choose one decision: study alone or with a group; cook at home or eat out; travel by bus or taxi. Speak for 60–90 seconds:
- state your choice;
- compare both options;
- give one advantage and one drawback;
- explain one likely consequence;
- say what could change your decision.

Use keywords, not a complete script. Ask a tutor or AI partner to challenge one point, then respond.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without notes, use at least three comparison expressions, explain one consequence, and answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable comparison. Otherwise mark **Review** and retry with a simpler pair of options.
