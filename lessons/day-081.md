# Day 081 — Week 11 review

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 25–35 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** review professional introductions, networking questions, and meeting clarification, then choose a skill to strengthen.

This is a lighter review day, not a new lesson or automatic pass.

## 1. Retrieval — 5 minutes

Without notes, say one phrase for each purpose:
- introduce yourself professionally;
- ask about another person's work;
- ask a follow-up question;
- clarify a deadline;
- summarise an agreement.

Check your notes afterwards and mark which phrases you retrieved independently.

## 2. Short listening — 10 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this exchange twice without showing it first.

> Manager: Can you send the summary by Wednesday?  
> Alex: Just to confirm, do you need it before noon?  
> Manager: Yes, by 11 a.m. is ideal.  
> Alex: Understood. I'll send the summary by 10:30. Do you also need the detailed table?  
> Manager: No, the summary is enough for this meeting.  
> Alex: Great. I'll send the summary by 10:30 on Wednesday.

Questions:
1. What does the manager ask Alex to send?
2. What deadline does Alex confirm?
3. Does the manager need the detailed table?
4. What does Alex promise to do?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 3. Speaking review — 10–15 minutes

Role-play a professional conversation for two minutes. Introduce yourself, ask about the other person's work, ask one connected follow-up, and confirm a task or appointment detail. If practising alone, record a two-sided role-play and label it as solo practice.

## 4. Mastery decision — 5 minutes

- **Ready:** you can introduce yourself, ask relevant questions, and confirm a detail without reading a full script.
- **Review:** one of those skills still needs practice; repeat that task and record a correction.
- **Not assessed:** no oral attempt or no listening evidence when listening is required.

Record one strength and one next target. This review does not automatically approve the week or phase.
