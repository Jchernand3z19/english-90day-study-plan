# Day 032 — Reservar hotel y habitación

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** make a hotel reservation by specifying arrival date, length of stay, room type, and asking about price or check-in.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Hotel reservations: useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **I'd like to book a room for two nights.** | Quisiera reservar una habitación por dos noches. |
| **Do you have a single or double room?** | ¿Tiene habitación individual o doble? |
| **Is breakfast included?** | ¿Está incluido el desayuno? |
| **What time is check-in?** | ¿A qué hora es el registro de entrada? |
| **Could you confirm the total price?** | ¿Podría confirmar el precio total? |
| **I'd like to cancel/change my booking.** | Quisiera cancelar/cambiar mi reserva. |

Practise as a guest and as a receptionist. Give the dates, number of nights, room type, and one question about the booking. Use invented details only as role-play.


## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this hotel-booking conversation twice without showing you the script first.

> Guest: Hello. I'd like to book a room for two nights.
>
> Receptionist: Certainly. What date would you like to arrive?
>
> Guest: Friday, November 6th.
>
> Receptionist: Would you prefer a single or double room?
>
> Guest: A double room, please. Is breakfast included?
>
> Receptionist: Yes, breakfast is included. Check-in starts at three p.m.

**Questions**
1. How many nights does the guest want to stay?
2. What date will the guest arrive?
3. Which room does the guest request?
4. What time does check-in start?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes

**Main task:** Reservar hotel y habitación. Explain or perform the situation aloud for 1–3 minutes, depending on difficulty. Use keywords rather than reading a complete script.

Answer these prompts:
1. What would you say first in this situation?
2. What detail or reason should you include?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat once with less support. An AI tutor can present one prompt at a time and give corrections in Spanish after you answer.
