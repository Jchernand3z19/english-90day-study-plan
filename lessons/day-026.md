# Day 026 — Frequency and healthy routines

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** describe how often you do activities and ask about routines using frequency expressions.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it and record only the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| How often do you exercise? | ¿Con qué frecuencia hace ejercicio? |
| I usually walk after work. | Normalmente camino después del trabajo. |
| Twice a week. | Dos veces por semana. |
| Most days. | La mayoría de los días. |
| Sometimes I eat out. | A veces como fuera. |
| How often do you cook at home? | ¿Con qué frecuencia cocina en casa? |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and use them in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Leo: Do you exercise during the week?  
Nina: Yes, I usually walk after work.  
Leo: How often do you go to the gym?  
Nina: Twice a week, on Tuesday and Saturday.  
Leo: Do you cook at home?  
Nina: Most days, but I sometimes eat out on Friday.

**Questions**
1. What does Nina usually do after work?  
2. How often does she go to the gym?  
3. Which two days does she go?  
4. When does she sometimes eat out?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Describe how often you do three activities using usually, sometimes, once a week, or twice a week. Then ask two questions about another person's routine.

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
