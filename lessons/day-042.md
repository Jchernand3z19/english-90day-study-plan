# Day 042 — Preguntar por experiencias de otros

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask someone about a past experience, listen to details, and ask a relevant follow-up question.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult cards and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Have you ever been to…?** | ¿Ha estado alguna vez en…? |
| **When did you go?** | ¿Cuándo fue? |
| **Who did you go with?** | ¿Con quién fue? |
| **What was it like?** | ¿Cómo fue? |
| **What did you enjoy most?** | ¿Qué disfrutó más? |
| **Would you recommend it?** | ¿Lo recomendaría? |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Create three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Sam: Have you ever been to Guatemala?
> Luisa: Yes, I went two years ago with my family.
> Sam: What was it like?
> Luisa: We visited Antigua. The streets were beautiful, and the food was excellent.
> Sam: What did you enjoy most?
> Luisa: I enjoyed walking around the old town. I'd definitely recommend it.

**Questions**
1. When did Luisa go?
2. Who did she travel with?
3. Which place did they visit?
4. What did she enjoy most?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Ask a partner or AI tutor about a trip, a meal, a film, or a new activity. Ask at least four questions, including one follow-up based on the answer. Then switch roles and answer questions about a real or clearly labelled fictional experience.

Use keywords, not a full script. Ask for feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask four relevant questions.
- Ask at least one follow-up based on a detail.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review** and practise the weak skill again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
