# Day 025 — Work and daily activities

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** Describe a typical workday using true information.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Practice these expressions. Read the English first, explain the meaning in Spanish, then say the English phrase without looking.

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| I prepare reports. | Preparo informes. |
| I analyze data. | Analizo datos. |
| I attend meetings. | Asisto a reuniones. |
| My main responsibility is… | Mi responsabilidad principal es… |

**Practice tip:** learn each expression as a complete chunk. Focus on meaning and use before trying to memorize grammar terminology. Use your own real information; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Ana: What do you do at work?
>
> Ben: I prepare reports and answer questions from my team.
>
> Ana: What time do you start?
>
> Ben: I start at eight and finish at five.
>
> Ana: What do you do after work?
>
> Ben: I usually go for a walk or study English.

**Questions**
1. What does Ben do at work?
2. What time does he start?
3. What time does he finish?
4. What does he usually do after work?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production practice — 15 minutes

**Role-play: explain a typical workday.** Describe when you start and finish, name two tasks you really do or clearly label them as invented practice, and say which task you do most often. Your partner asks *What time do you…?* and *How often do you…?* Then switch roles and ask the partner two questions about their work or daily activities. Repeat using only keywords and note two phrases you produced without help.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Produce at least three of today's target phrases in English.
- Use two phrases in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of the phrases you used.
- Ask for clarification in English if you get stuck.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the self-check items are completed correctly and the main speaking task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, an attempted oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.

## Evidence to record

Create or update the daily log for Day 025:
- Date and actual time spent.
- phrases recalled without help and phrases needing review.
- Whether listening/shadowing was actually completed and which source was used.
- Speaking task attempted and what you could say without reading.
- Self-check score and two corrections.
- Next review target.



## Completion rule

Keep the status **Pending** until you actually complete the work. Use **In progress** if you started, **Review** if you need more practice, and **Approved** only after the task, self-check, and evidence are complete.
