# Day 058 — Repaso ligero / descanso

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** refresh travel and social-conversation phrases without adding heavy new material, and choose what to practise before the Phase 2 checkpoint.

This is intentionally lighter than a normal lesson. A full rest day is fine; if you rest, leave the lesson pending rather than counting it as completed practice.

## 1. Quick retrieval — 5–8 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Retrieve useful phrases — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, say one phrase for each purpose:

| Purpose | Example | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm a reservation | I'd like to confirm my reservation. | Quisiera confirmar mi reserva. |
| Ask for help | Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |
| Explain a problem | There's a problem with my booking. | Hay un problema con mi reserva. |
| Tell a past event | Last weekend, I went to… | El fin de semana pasado fui a… |
| Continue a conversation | What happened next? | ¿Qué pasó después? |
| Clarify | Could you say that again, please? | ¿Podría repetirlo, por favor? |

Choose three phrases and adapt them to a realistic situation. Say each one twice without reading.

## 3. Mini role-play — 5–8 minutes

Choose one:
- explain a problem with a hotel booking;
- ask for directions or a nearby service;
- tell someone about a recent experience and ask them a question.

Speak for 30–60 seconds. Focus on communicating the main message. If a tutor or AI is available, ask for one correction in Spanish and repeat the corrected sentence.

## 4. Plan the checkpoint — 2 minutes

Write down:
- one skill that feels reliable;
- one skill that still needs practice;
- one question or phrase to review before Day 59.

No score is required today. Use evidence, not confidence alone, to choose the review target.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, phrases practised, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working and **Approved** only after completing this light review. A full rest day remains pending.
