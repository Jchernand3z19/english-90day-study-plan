# Day 033 — Aeropuerto y transporte

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask for transport information at an airport and confirm the gate, departure time, or destination.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Where is the check-in desk?** | ¿Dónde está el mostrador de facturación? |
| **Which gate does my flight leave from?** | ¿De qué puerta sale mi vuelo? |
| **What time does boarding start?** | ¿A qué hora empieza el embarque? |
| **Is this the bus to the city centre?** | ¿Este es el autobús al centro? |
| **Where can I buy a ticket?** | ¿Dónde puedo comprar un billete? |
| **How long does the journey take?** | ¿Cuánto dura el trayecto? |

Say each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish meaning and try to retrieve it. Make three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Passenger: Excuse me, where is the check-in desk for Flight 208?
> Staff: It's at Desk 14. Boarding starts at 10:20 at Gate 6.
> Passenger: Thank you. Is there a bus to the city centre from here?
> Staff: Yes. The bus stop is outside Terminal 1. The journey takes about thirty minutes.
> Passenger: Great. Where can I buy a ticket?
> Staff: You can buy one from the machine beside the exit.

**Questions**
1. Which desk does the passenger need?
2. What time does boarding start?
3. Which gate is mentioned?
4. Where can the passenger buy a bus ticket?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play asking airport staff about a check-in desk, gate, boarding time, and transport to the city. Repeat the key information in your own words to confirm it.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask two relevant airport or transport questions.
- Confirm a time and a location.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable oral production. If the core task still requires reading a full script, mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
