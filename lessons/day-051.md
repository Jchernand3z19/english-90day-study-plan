# Day 051 — Repaso ligero / descanso

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** refresh useful phrases for travel problems and social conversation without adding heavy new material.

This is intentionally lighter than a normal lesson. A full rest day is fine; if you rest, leave the lesson pending.

## 1. Quick retrieval — 5–10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Phrase review — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, say a phrase for each purpose:

| Purpose | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Ask for help | Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |
| Explain a problem | There's a problem with my booking. | Hay un problema con mi reserva. |
| Ask for an alternative | Is there another option? | ¿Hay otra opción? |
| Accept an invitation | That sounds great. | Suena muy bien. |
| Decline politely | I'd love to, but I can't. | Me encantaría, pero no puedo. |
| Continue a conversation | What happened next? | ¿Qué pasó después? |

Choose three phrases and use them in a short role-play.

## 3. Mini speaking review — 5–8 minutes

Choose one scenario: ask for help with a booking, explain an unexpected problem, or respond to an invitation. Speak for 30–60 seconds, then ask one follow-up question. If a tutor or AI is available, request one correction in Spanish and repeat the corrected sentence.

## 4. Choose a target — 2 minutes

Record one phrase you remembered easily and one phrase or skill that still needs practice. No score is required today.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, phrases recalled without help, phrases practised, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; a full rest day remains pending and does not count as completed practice.
