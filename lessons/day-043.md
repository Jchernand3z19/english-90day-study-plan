# Day 043 — Narrar una anécdota breve

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** tell a short anecdote with a beginning, ordered events, and a closing reaction.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **You won't believe what happened.** | No vas a creer lo que pasó. |
| **At first,…** | Al principio… |
| **Suddenly,…** | De repente… |
| **Luckily,…** | Por suerte… |
| **In the end,…** | Al final… |
| **I was surprised because…** | Me sorprendí porque… |
| **It was a funny / stressful experience.** | Fue una experiencia divertida / estresante. |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Create three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Mia: You won't believe what happened on my way home.
> Jay: What happened?
> Mia: At first, I thought I'd missed the bus. Suddenly, the driver stopped across the street and waved at me.
> Jay: That's lucky!
> Mia: Yes. Luckily, I got on just in time. In the end, I arrived home only five minutes late.
> Jay: What a story!

**Questions**
1. Where was Mia going?
2. What did she think at first?
3. What did the driver do?
4. How late was she in the end?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Tell a 60–90 second anecdote about a small surprise, a mistake, or a lucky moment. Use **at first**, **suddenly**, **luckily**, and **in the end** where appropriate. A fictional story is fine if you label it as role-play. Answer one follow-up question.

Use keywords, not a full script. Ask for feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Organise the story in a clear order.
- Use at least three sequence or reaction expressions.
- Answer a follow-up question.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review** and practise the weak skill again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
