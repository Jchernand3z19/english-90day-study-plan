# Day 038 — Pasado simple: verbos frecuentes

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use frequent past-simple verbs to describe a completed event in a clear, short story.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 3–5 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Past-simple verbs — 15 minutes

For regular verbs, the past form often ends in **-ed**: work → worked, visit → visited. Several common verbs are irregular and must be learned as a pair.

| Base form | Past simple | Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| go | went | ir / fui | I went to the store. |
| have | had | tener / tuve | We had lunch at noon. |
| see | saw | ver / vi | I saw an old friend. |
| buy | bought | comprar / compré | She bought a ticket. |
| meet | met | conocer / me reuní con | I met my cousin downtown. |
| make | made | hacer / hice | He made dinner. |
| take | took | tomar / llevar | We took a taxi. |
| get | got | conseguir / llegar / recibir | I got home at six. |
| visit | visited | visitar / visité | They visited a museum. |
| watch | watched | ver / mirar | I watched a film. |

**Key rule:** in a positive sentence, use the past form: **I went**. In a question with **did**, use the base form: **Did you go?** Not “Did you went?” For a negative, use **didn't + base form**: **I didn't go**.

**Practice**
1. Say the past form of all ten verbs.
2. Complete aloud: “Yesterday, I ___ (go) …”; “Last weekend, I ___ (see) …”; “I didn't ___ (buy) …”
3. Make three true sentences. If you use an invented event, label it as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice **without showing you the script first**. Then answer the questions aloud. If studying alone, cover the script while listening.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Eva: What did you do on Saturday?  
> Mark: I went downtown and met a friend.  
> Eva: Did you have lunch together?  
> Mark: Yes. We had lunch at a small café, and then we saw a film.  
> Eva: Did you take a taxi home?  
> Mark: No, I took the bus. I got home at seven.

**Questions**
1. Where did Mark go?
2. Who did he meet?
3. What did they do after lunch?
4. How did Mark get home?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading the script is not listening evidence. Check the script after answering, then shadow two lines and notice how **did** is followed by the base verb.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Build a very short past-event description using the model. Start with two sentences; aim for about 20–30 seconds. Include:
- when it happened;
- where you went or stayed;
- one or two actions using past-simple verbs;
- one sequence word: **first**, **then**, **after that**, or **finally**.

Then answer aloud: **“What didn't go as planned?”** You may say “Nothing went wrong” if that is true. An AI tutor can ask one follow-up question at a time and correct your response in Spanish. Retry two corrected sentences without reading.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Recall at least six past forms correctly.
- Make one positive sentence, one question with **did**, and one negative with **didn't**.
- Answer at least three of the four listening questions correctly.
- Complete the short oral story.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 using the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable story. If you confuse **did + base form**, mark **Review** and practise that pattern before advancing.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, story keywords, score, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
