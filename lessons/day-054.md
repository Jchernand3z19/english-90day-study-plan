# Day 054 — Intereses y actividades

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** talk about hobbies and free-time activities, explain a preference, and ask a follow-up question.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes
Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Interests and activities — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **What do you enjoy doing in your free time?** | ¿Qué disfruta hacer en su tiempo libre? |
| **I'm really into…** | Me gusta mucho… |
| **I enjoy… because…** | Disfruto… porque… |
| **I'm not really interested in…** | No me interesa mucho… |
| **I'd rather…** | Preferiría… |
| **How did you get interested in that?** | ¿Cómo empezó a interesarse en eso? |
| **How often do you do it?** | ¿Con qué frecuencia lo hace? |

Say three true sentences about your interests. If you prefer not to share personal details, use a clearly labelled fictional character. Then ask two follow-up questions about another person's interests.


## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this conversation twice without showing you the script first.

> Leo: What do you like doing in your free time?
>
> Sara: I enjoy hiking and trying new recipes.
>
> Leo: Which do you prefer?
>
> Sara: Hiking, because I like being outdoors.
>
> Leo: How often do you go?
>
> Sara: Usually twice a month, when the weather is good.

**Questions**
1. What two activities does Sara enjoy?
2. Which activity does she prefer?
3. Why does she prefer it?
4. How often does she usually go hiking?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes

**Role-play: discover shared interests.** Tell a partner two activities you enjoy and one you do not enjoy. Ask what the partner likes doing, how often they do it, and one follow-up question about why or when. React with a phrase that shows interest, then suggest one activity you could do together. Switch roles and repeat using different interests.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes
Without looking at notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain what your examples mean.
- Ask one follow-up or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. Speaking-focused tasks require an actual oral attempt. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.

## Evidence and completion
Record the date, actual time spent, phrase retrieval, audio source if used, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target in a daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until completed. Use **In progress** if started, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when the work and evidence are complete.
