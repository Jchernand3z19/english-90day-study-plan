# Day 054 — Intereses y actividades

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** practice **intereses y actividades** in useful, understandable English.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes
Review due cards from the 1,000 common English phrases deck. Try to recall each phrase aloud before revealing the answer. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts; do not estimate progress.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes
Practice these expressions and adapt them to today's situation:

| English | Use |
|---|---|
| Could you confirm that, please? | Ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| What happened next? | Ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| Could you help me with this? | Ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| Is there another option? | Ask, explain, respond, or clarify in today's situation. |
| Let me think for a moment. | Gain time to organize an answer. |
| What I mean is… | Explain an idea more clearly. |

Create three examples connected to today's topic. Explain their meaning in Spanish, then say each English sentence without looking. Use true personal information or clearly marked role-play details.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this conversation twice without showing you the script first.

> Leo: What do you like doing in your free time?
>
> Sara: I enjoy hiking and trying new recipes.
>
> Leo: Which do you prefer?
>
> Sara: Hiking, because I like being outdoors.
>
> Leo: How often do you go?
>
> Sara: Usually twice a month, when the weather is good.

**Questions**
1. What two activities does Sara enjoy?
2. Which activity does she prefer?
3. Why does she prefer it?
4. How often does she usually go hiking?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes
**Main task:** Intereses y actividades. Speak or role-play for 1–3 minutes, using keywords rather than a complete written script.

Then answer aloud:
1. What would you say first?
2. What important detail or reason should you include?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat once with less support. If using an AI tutor, request one prompt at a time and corrections in Spanish after your response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes
Without looking at notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain what your examples mean.
- Ask one follow-up or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. Speaking-focused tasks require an actual oral attempt. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
