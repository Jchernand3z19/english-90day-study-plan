# Day 005 — Small Talk: Weather and Interests

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** start a short conversation about the weather, free time, and interests.

## 1. Anki warm-up — 10 minutes

Review phrases from previous days. Try to recall five before checking the answer.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| How's the weather today? | ¿Cómo está el clima hoy? |
| It's sunny. | Está soleado. |
| It's cloudy. | Está nublado. |
| It's raining. | Está lloviendo. |
| It's really hot today. | Hoy hace mucho calor. |
| What do you do in your free time? | ¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre? |
| I like listening to music. | Me gusta escuchar música. |
| I'm into movies. | Me gustan mucho las películas. |
| That sounds interesting. | Eso suena interesante. |
| Really? Tell me more. | ¿En serio? Cuéntame más. |
| How about you? | ¿Y tú? |
| What kind of music do you like? | ¿Qué tipo de música te gusta? |

### Keep the conversation going

Instead of only saying “yes” or “no,” add one detail and ask a question back.

Example:
- “I like movies. I usually watch action movies. How about you?”

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

A: It's really hot today, isn't it?  
B: Yes, it is. I prefer cooler weather.  
A: What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music and watching movies. How about you?  
A: I enjoy going for walks.

Repeat the dialogue and practice the rhythm of the complete phrases. If you use an audio source, record which one in your daily log.

## 4. Speak — 15 minutes

Answer aloud:

1. How's the weather today?
2. What's your favorite kind of weather?
3. What do you do in your free time?
4. What kind of music or movies do you like?
5. What is one activity you would like to try?

Ask at least two follow-up questions as if you were talking to another person.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking, say:

1. Hoy hace mucho calor.
2. ¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre?
3. Me gusta escuchar música.
4. Eso suena interesante.
5. ¿Y tú?

**Suggested pass:** produce 4 of 5 phrases understandably and sustain a short exchange with at least one follow-up question.

## Evidence

Record the phrases you recalled, your answers to the speaking prompts, and one question that helped continue the conversation. Mark the session approved only after completing the practice and check.
