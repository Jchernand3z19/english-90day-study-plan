# Day 005 — Small talk: weather and interests

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** make small talk about weather and free-time interests, then ask a simple follow-up question.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| It's hot/cool/rainy today. | Hace calor/fresco/está lluvioso hoy. |
| What do you like to do in your free time? | ¿Qué te gusta hacer en tu tiempo libre? |
| I like listening to music. | Me gusta escuchar música. |
| I enjoy going for walks. | Disfruto salir a caminar. |
| How about you? | ¿Y tú/usted? |
| Really? | ¿En serio? |

Read each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish column and try to recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and say them without looking.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

A: It's really hot today, isn't it?  
B: Yes, it is. I prefer cooler weather.  
A: What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music and watching movies. How about you?  
A: I enjoy going for walks.

**Questions**
1. How is the weather?  
2. What does B like doing?  
3. What does A enjoy doing?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then repeat two lines, imitating rhythm and pronunciation. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Speak for 30 seconds about today's weather or an interest. Ask one question and respond to the answer. Hear the dialogue first; if you cannot access audio, mark listening not assessed.

If a tutor or AI tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish. Repeat the corrected sentence. Try the task again with less help.
