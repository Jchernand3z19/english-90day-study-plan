## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing it first. Answer the questions, then check the text and practise the rhythm of complete phrases.

A: It's really hot today, isn't it?  
B: Yes, it is. I prefer cooler weather.  
A: What do you like to do in your free time?  
B: I like listening to music and watching movies. How about you?  
A: I enjoy going for walks.

**Questions**
1. How is the weather?
2. What does Speaker B like doing?
3. What does Speaker A enjoy doing?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**. If you use an audio source, record which one in your daily log.
