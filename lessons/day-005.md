# Day 005 — Say one thing you like

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 40–50 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** express one preference with “I like…” and ask another person about an interest.

## 1. Recall — 5 minutes

Say “My name is…” and “I'm from…”. Then say “Hi” and “How are you?”

## 2. Learn the chunks — 10–15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| I like music. | Me gusta la música. |
| I like listening to music. | Me gusta escuchar música. |
| What do you like? | ¿Qué te gusta? |
| I like movies. | Me gustan las películas. |
| How about you? | ¿Y tú? |

For now, learn the complete examples as phrases. You do not need a grammar explanation of every verb form. Repeat each phrase after hearing it.

## 3. Guided listening — 10 minutes

Listen twice without reading:

> A: What do you like?  
> B: I like music.  
> A: I like movies.  
> B: How about you?  
> A: I like listening to music.

Answer:
1. What does B like?
2. What does A like?
3. Which phrase asks the other person?

If audio was not played, mark listening **Not assessed**. Reveal the script only after listening.

## 4. Supported speaking — 10–15 minutes

Choose one true answer:
- I like music.
- I like movies.
- I like ______.

Then practise:
- A: What do you like?
- B: I like ______.
- A: How about you?

You may read the model. Repeat your own answer twice, slowly and then naturally.

## 5. Check — 5 minutes

Say “I like…” plus one word or phrase. Ask “What do you like?” while looking at the model. Record which version you can say independently.

**Completion rule:** no 30-second speech is expected. Do not add weather or several interests yet.
