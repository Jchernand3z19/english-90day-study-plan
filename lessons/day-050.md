# Day 050 — Role-play de un imprevisto

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** respond to an unexpected travel or service problem by explaining what happened, asking for help, and confirming a solution.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record actual counts.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **My bus has been cancelled.** | Han cancelado mi autobús. |
| **I think I've lost my ticket.** | Creo que he perdido mi billete. |
| **What are my options?** | ¿Qué opciones tengo? |
| **Could you help me rebook?** | ¿Podría ayudarme a cambiar la reserva? |
| **Is there another service today?** | ¿Hay otro servicio hoy? |
| **How long will I have to wait?** | ¿Cuánto tendré que esperar? |
| **Just to confirm,…** | Solo para confirmar… |

Practise each phrase aloud. Then cover the Spanish and retrieve at least five meanings.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Traveller: Excuse me, my bus to the city has been cancelled.
>
> Staff: I'm sorry. There's another bus at 3:30 p.m.
>
> Traveller: What are my options if I need to arrive earlier?
>
> Staff: You can take a train at 2:45, but it costs a little more.
>
> Traveller: I see. How long does the train journey take?
>
> Staff: About forty minutes.
>
> Traveller: Just to confirm, the train leaves at 2:45 and arrives around 3:25?
>
> Staff: That's correct.

**Questions**
1. What happened to the traveller's bus?
2. What time is the next bus?
3. What earlier alternative is available?
4. How long does the train journey take?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Choose one unexpected situation:
- your bus or flight is cancelled;
- your hotel cannot find your booking;
- you lose a ticket or cannot find a location;
- an appointment is moved unexpectedly.

Explain the problem in two sentences, ask for two options, compare them, and confirm the chosen solution. The other speaker should ask one follow-up question. A fictional role-play is fine; label it as such.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Explain what happened clearly.
- Ask about at least two options or a solution.
- Repeat one key detail to confirm it.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable role-play. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, role-play situation, score, corrections, and next review target.
