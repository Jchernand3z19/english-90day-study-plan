# Day 047 — Pedir ayuda y soluciones

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask for help, explain what you need, and respond to a suggested solution.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Could you help me with…?** | ¿Podría ayudarme con…? |
| **I'm having trouble with…** | Tengo problemas con… |
| **Could you show me how to…?** | ¿Podría mostrarme cómo…? |
| **What do you suggest?** | ¿Qué me recomienda? |
| **Would it be possible to…?** | ¿Sería posible…? |
| **That would help a lot.** | Eso ayudaría mucho. |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Visitor: Excuse me, I'm having trouble using the ticket machine.
> Staff: I can help. What ticket do you need?
> Visitor: A ticket to the city centre. Could you show me how to buy one?
> Staff: Of course. Choose the destination, then select card or cash.
> Visitor: I see. Would it be possible to print a receipt too?
> Staff: Yes, just select “receipt” before paying.
> Visitor: Thank you. That would help a lot.

**Questions**
1. What is the visitor having trouble with?
2. Where does the visitor want to go?
3. What are the payment options?
4. When should the visitor select “receipt”?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play asking for help with a machine, an unfamiliar process, or a service. Explain the difficulty, ask for a demonstration, and check the instructions by repeating them in your own words.

Use keywords rather than a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Explain what help you need.
- Ask one follow-up question.
- Repeat the instructions in your own words.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review** and practise the weak skill again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
