# Day 059 — Evaluación integrada de fase 2

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** demonstrate practical travel, storytelling, problem-solving, and social conversation skills from Phase 2.

This checkpoint uses the fixed task weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md`. It is not an official CEFR exam.

## 1. Prepare — 5 minutes

Review your Phase 2 notes briefly, then close them. Do not read prepared answers during the speaking tasks. Instructions and feedback can be in Spanish; attempt the tasks in English.

## 2. Task A — Travel scenario (10 minutes, 20 points)

Role-play confirming a fictional hotel or transport reservation. Ask about or confirm the date, time, and one important detail. Use phrases such as **I'd like to confirm my reservation**, **What time does it leave?**, and **Could you repeat the confirmation number, please?**

## 3. Task B — Past experience (12 minutes, 25 points)

Tell a short story for about 45–60 seconds. Include a beginning, two past actions, one or two sequence words (**first**, **then**, **after that**, **finally**), and an ending. You may use a clearly labelled fictional story if you do not want to share a personal one. Be ready to answer one follow-up question.

## 4. Task C — Unexpected problem (10 minutes, 20 points)

Explain a travel or service problem in one or two sentences, request help, and ask about one possible solution. Example: **My room reservation isn't in the system. Could you check it, please? Is there another room available?** The other speaker should respond and ask one question.

## 5. Task D — Social conversation (13 minutes, 20 points)

Maintain a guided conversation for about 2 minutes about a familiar topic or recent experience. Answer questions and ask two relevant follow-up questions, such as **How was it?**, **What happened next?**, and **Would you do it again?**

## 6. Task E — Listening and repair (10 minutes, 15 points)

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this travel dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Guest: Excuse me, I booked a room for tonight, but I can't find the confirmation email.
>
> Receptionist: No problem. Could I have your name, please?
>
> Guest: It's Taylor. Could you repeat the room details when you find them?
>
> Receptionist: Certainly. You booked a double room for one night, with breakfast included.
>
> Guest: Thank you. So, breakfast is included and check-out is at eleven, right?
>
> Receptionist: That's correct.

**Questions**
1. What problem does the guest have?
2. What kind of room did the guest book?
3. Is breakfast included?
4. What time is check-out?

Answer before checking the script, then use a repair phrase in a brief role-play: **Sorry, could you say that again?** or **Do you mean…?** If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark this task **Not assessed** and the overall checkpoint **Incomplete**. Do not replace listening with reading aloud.

## Scoring and next step

Use the fixed weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md` (20 + 25 + 20 + 20 + 15 = 100). Suggested pass: at least 80/100, with every task attempted and the main message understandable. Record the actual score and evidence for each task.

If below 80, mark **Review**, identify the two weakest skills, and practise them on Day 60. Day 60 is for targeted corrections and comparison with Phase 1 evidence, not an automatic pass.
