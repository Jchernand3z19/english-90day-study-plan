# Day 059 — Evaluación integrada de fase 2

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** demonstrate practical travel, storytelling, problem-solving, and social conversation skills from Phase 2.

This checkpoint uses the fixed task weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md`. It is not an official CEFR exam.

## 1. Prepare — 5 minutes

Review your Phase 2 notes briefly, then close them. Do not read prepared answers during the speaking tasks. Instructions and feedback can be in Spanish; attempt the tasks in English.

## 2. Task A — Travel scenario (10 minutes, 20 points)

Role-play confirming a fictional hotel or transport reservation. Ask about or confirm the date, time, and one important detail. Use phrases such as **I'd like to confirm my reservation**, **What time does it leave?**, and **Could you repeat the confirmation number, please?**

## 3. Task B — Past experience (15 minutes, 25 points)

Tell a story for 1–2 minutes. Include a beginning, at least three past actions, sequence words (**first**, **then**, **after that**, **finally**), and an ending. You may use a clearly labelled fictional story if you do not want to share a personal one. Be ready to answer one follow-up question.

## 4. Task C — Unexpected problem (10 minutes, 20 points)

Explain a travel or service problem, request help, and discuss one possible solution. Example: **My room reservation isn't in the system. Could you check it, please? Is there another room available?** The other speaker should respond and ask one question.

## 5. Task D — Social conversation (10 minutes, 20 points)

Maintain a guided conversation for 3–5 minutes about a familiar topic or recent experience. Answer questions and ask at least three relevant follow-up questions, such as **How was it?**, **What happened next?**, and **Would you do it again?**

## 6. Task E — Listening and repair (10 minutes, 15 points)

Have a tutor or text-to-speech tool read a short travel or social dialogue twice without showing the text. State its main idea and one detail, then use a repair phrase in a role-play: **Sorry, could you say that again?** or **Do you mean…?**

If listening cannot be performed, mark it **Not assessed** and the overall checkpoint **Incomplete**. Do not replace listening with reading aloud.

## Scoring and next step

Use the fixed weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md` (20 + 25 + 20 + 20 + 15 = 100). Suggested pass: at least 80/100, with every task attempted and the main message understandable. Record the actual score and evidence for each task.

If below 80, mark **Review**, identify the two weakest skills, and practise them on Day 60. Day 60 is for targeted corrections and comparison with Phase 1 evidence, not an automatic pass.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, task responses, listening method, score by task, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep this lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the assessment rules.
