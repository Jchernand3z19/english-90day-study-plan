# Day 019 — Distance and location

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** Compare two locations and explain how to get there.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Practice these expressions. Read the English first, explain the meaning in Spanish, then say the English phrase without looking.

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Is it far from here? | ¿Está lejos de aquí? |
| It's about ten minutes away. | Está a unos diez minutos. |
| It's within walking distance. | Se puede llegar caminando. |
| How far is the airport? | ¿A qué distancia está el aeropuerto? |

**Practice tip:** learn each expression as a complete chunk. Focus on meaning and use before trying to memorize grammar terminology. Use your own real information; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Tourist: Is the museum far from here?
>
> Local: No, it's about five hundred metres away.
>
> Tourist: How do I get there?
>
> Local: Walk past the bank. The museum is between the library and the park.
>
> Tourist: Is it on the left or the right?
>
> Local: It's on your right, just after the library.

**Questions**
1. Is the museum far away?
2. Which place should the tourist walk past?
3. What two places is the museum between?
4. Which side is the museum on?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production practice — 15 minutes

Practise the two models: “It's about ten minutes away” and “It's within walking distance.” Choose one and use it to answer “Is it far?” Comparing two routes is optional extension practice.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Produce at least three of today's target phrases in English.
- Use two phrases in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of the phrases you used.
- Ask for clarification in English if you get stuck.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the self-check items are completed correctly and the main speaking task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, an attempted oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
