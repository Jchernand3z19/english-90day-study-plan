# Day 077 — Networking questions

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask open-ended questions in a professional conversation and respond with a relevant follow-up.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **What kind of projects do you work on?** | ¿En qué tipo de proyectos trabaja? |
| **How did you get started in this field?** | ¿Cómo empezó en este campo? |
| **What do you enjoy most about your work?** | ¿Qué es lo que más disfruta de su trabajo? |
| **What skills are important in your role?** | ¿Qué habilidades son importantes en su puesto? |
| **What are you working on at the moment?** | ¿En qué está trabajando actualmente? |
| **Could you tell me more about that?** | ¿Podría contarme más sobre eso? |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples and explain their meaning in Spanish.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Ana: What kind of projects do you work on?
> Luis: Mostly reporting projects. I help teams understand their performance.
> Ana: What do you enjoy most about that?
> Luis: I like finding patterns in the data and explaining them clearly.
> Ana: What skills are important in your role?
> Luis: Attention to detail and communication are both important.

**Questions**
1. What kind of projects does Luis work on?
2. What does he enjoy?
3. Name one skill he says is important.
4. What does Ana ask after his first answer?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play a networking conversation. Ask three open-ended questions, listen to the answers, and ask one follow-up that uses a detail from the previous answer. Share one truthful detail about your own work or interests.

Use keywords, not a complete script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask three open-ended questions.
- Ask one connected follow-up question.
- Share one relevant truthful detail.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. If you still need a full script, mark **Review** and repeat the task with fewer notes.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
