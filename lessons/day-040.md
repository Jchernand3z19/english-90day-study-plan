# Day 040 — Ordenar eventos en una historia

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** retell a short event in chronological order using sequence phrases such as *first*, *then*, *after that*, and *in the end*.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Sequence a story — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **First,…** | Primero,… |
| **Then,…** | Después,… |
| **After that,…** | Después de eso,… |
| **A little later,…** | Un poco más tarde,… |
| **Suddenly,…** | De repente,… |
| **In the end,…** | Al final,… |
| **Because of that,…** | Por eso,… |

Put these events in order and tell the story aloud using at least five sequence phrases:

1. I missed the bus.
2. I called a friend.
3. My friend picked me up.
4. We arrived just in time.
5. I thanked my friend.

Then tell a real short story from your life, or label it clearly as fictional. Aim for 5–7 connected sentences. Focus on order and clarity rather than complex grammar.


## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this story twice without showing you the script first.

> Nina: What happened yesterday?
>
> Omar: First, I missed the bus. Then I called my friend.
>
> Nina: What happened next?
>
> Omar: My friend picked me up. After that, we drove to the office.
>
> Nina: Did you arrive on time?
>
> Omar: Yes. In the end, we arrived just in time, so I thanked my friend.

**Questions**
1. What happened first?
2. Who did Omar call?
3. How did Omar get to the office?
4. Did they arrive on time?

Answer before checking the script. Then retell the story using **first, then, after that,** and **in the end**. If you did not actually hear it, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes

**Main task: retell a short story in order.** Use the five events from Section 2 or a short real event. Tell it in 5–7 connected sentences using at least four sequence phrases: *first*, *then*, *after that*, *a little later*, *in the end*. A tutor may ask what happened next or why. Retell the story once more with less support; do not read a complete script.


## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at your notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two phrases in original sentences or a dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of your examples.
- Ask one follow-up question or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, attempting the oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
