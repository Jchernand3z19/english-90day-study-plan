# Day 023 — Frequency and schedules

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** Describe three habits and ask someone about theirs.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Practice these expressions. Read the English first, explain the meaning in Spanish, then say the English phrase without looking.

| English | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| I usually wake up early. | Understand the phrase and use it in the day's situation. |
| I sometimes cook dinner. | Understand the phrase and use it in the day's situation. |
| I rarely watch TV. | Understand the phrase and use it in the day's situation. |
| How often do you exercise? | Understand the phrase and use it in the day's situation. |

**Practice tip:** learn each expression as a complete chunk. Focus on meaning and use before trying to memorize grammar terminology. Use your own real information; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Sam: How often do you go shopping?
>
> Nina: Usually once a week, on Saturday.
>
> Sam: Do you ever go during the week?
>
> Nina: Sometimes, if I need something.
>
> Sam: What time do you usually go?
>
> Nina: I usually go in the morning, around ten.

**Questions**
1. How often does Nina go shopping?
2. Which day does she usually go?
3. When does she sometimes go during the week?
4. What time does she usually go?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production practice — 15 minutes

Complete the task aloud: **Describe three habits and ask someone about theirs.**

Then answer these prompts without reading a full script:
1. What is the main thing you need to say in this situation?
2. Which phrase from today's list helps you say it?
3. What follow-up question could you ask?
4. How would you ask the other person to repeat or clarify something?

Repeat the task once using only keywords. If working with an AI tutor, ask it to present one prompt at a time and give corrections in Spanish after you answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table:
- Produce at least three of today's target phrases in English.
- Use two phrases in original sentences or a short dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of the phrases you used.
- Ask for clarification in English if you get stuck.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the self-check items are completed correctly and the main speaking task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, an attempted oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
