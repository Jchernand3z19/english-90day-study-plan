# Day 078 — Meetings: clarify and confirm agreements

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** clarify a point in a meeting, confirm responsibilities and deadlines, and summarise an agreement.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Could you clarify the deadline?** | ¿Podría aclarar la fecha límite? |
| **Just to confirm,…** | Solo para confirmar… |
| **So, I'll handle…** | Entonces, yo me encargaré de… |
| **Are we agreed on…?** | ¿Estamos de acuerdo en…? |
| **When do you need it by?** | ¿Para cuándo lo necesita? |
| **Let me summarise the next steps.** | Permítame resumir los próximos pasos. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Manager: Can you prepare the weekly report?
> Alex: Sure. Just to confirm, do you need it by Thursday morning?
> Manager: Yes, before 10 a.m.
> Alex: Understood. I'll prepare the report and send it by 9:30. Could you clarify whether you need the full data table too?
> Manager: A summary and the main table are enough.
> Alex: Great. Let me summarise the next steps: I'll send both by 9:30 on Thursday.

**Questions**
1. What task does the manager assign?
2. What is the deadline?
3. What does Alex ask to clarify?
4. What will Alex send?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play a short work or study meeting. Confirm the task, deadline, expected format, and who is responsible. Finish by summarising the agreement in two sentences.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask one clarifying question.
- Confirm a deadline and responsibility.
- Summarise the agreed next steps.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target.
