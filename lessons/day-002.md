## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

If possible, ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogues twice without showing them first. Answer the questions below, then check the text and repeat each dialogue three times. If you cannot hear the dialogue, practise speaking but mark listening **Not assessed**.

**Dialogue A**

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. How about you?  
A: I'm doing well.  
B: Nice!

**Dialogue B**

A: It was nice meeting you.  
B: Nice meeting you too.  
A: Have a good day!  
B: You too. See you later!

**Questions**
1. How does Speaker B answer “How are you?”
2. What does Speaker A say before ending Dialogue B?
3. Which phrase could you use to end a conversation politely?

Focus on complete chunks rather than translating every word.
