# Day 002 — Greetings and polite conversation

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** greet someone, ask how they are, and end a short exchange politely.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it. Record only the number you actually reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Hi / Hello. | Hola. |
| How are you? | ¿Cómo está(s)? |
| I'm fine, thanks. And you? | Estoy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú/usted? |
| Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto. |
| It was nice talking to you. | Fue un gusto conversar contigo/usted. |
| See you later. | Hasta luego. |
| Have a good day. | Que tenga(s) un buen día. |

Read each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish column and try to recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and say them without looking.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. How about you?  
A: I'm doing well. It was nice talking to you.  
B: You too. Have a good day!

**Questions**
1. How does B answer the greeting?  
2. What does A say before ending the conversation?  
3. Name one polite way to say goodbye.

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then repeat two lines, imitating rhythm and pronunciation. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Say hello, ask how the other person is, respond, and end the conversation. Repeat once with different answers. Use a tutor or text-to-speech to hear the dialogue before reading it.

If a tutor or AI tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish. Repeat the corrected sentence. Try the task again with less help.
