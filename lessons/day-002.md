# Day 002 — Greetings and Goodbyes

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** greet someone, respond naturally, and end a short conversation politely.

## 1. Anki warm-up — 10 minutes

Review familiar phrases and try to recall them before revealing the answer. Choose five phrases you find difficult and say each aloud.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Hi! / Hello! | ¡Hola! |
| Good morning. | Buenos días. |
| Good afternoon. | Buenas tardes. |
| Good evening. | Buenas noches, al saludar. |
| How are you? | ¿Cómo estás? |
| I'm good, thanks. How about you? | Estoy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú? |
| Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto. |
| Nice to meet you too. | Igualmente. |
| See you later. | Nos vemos más tarde. |
| Have a good day. | Que tengas un buen día. |
| Take care. | Cuídate. |
| I'm doing well, thanks. | Estoy bien, gracias. |

**Useful note:** “Good night” is usually used when saying goodbye at night or going to sleep, not as the usual greeting when first meeting someone.

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Read the dialogues slowly, then repeat them three times. If you use a text-to-speech or audio source, listen first and then imitate the speaker.

**Dialogue A**

A: Hi! How are you?  
B: I'm good, thanks. How about you?  
A: I'm doing well.  
B: Nice!

**Dialogue B**

A: It was nice meeting you.  
B: Nice meeting you too.  
A: Have a good day!  
B: You too. See you later!

Focus on speaking in complete chunks rather than translating each word.

## 4. Speak — 15 minutes

Practice these situations aloud:

1. Greet a coworker in the morning.
2. Meet someone for the first time.
3. Answer “How are you?” and ask the same question back.
4. End a conversation politely.
5. Say goodbye to someone you expect to see tomorrow.

Repeat the dialogue with the roles reversed. Try once without reading.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Say the English phrase for each meaning without looking:

1. Buenos días.
2. Estoy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?
3. Mucho gusto.
4. Igualmente.
5. Que tengas un buen día.
6. Nos vemos más tarde.

**Suggested pass:** at least 5 of 6 phrases are understandable and you can complete one short greeting dialogue without reading every line.

## Evidence

Record your result in a daily log: phrases recalled, dialogue attempted, phrases that were difficult, score, and what you need to review. Leave this session pending until you actually complete it.
