# Day 002 — Greetings and a simple reply

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 40–50 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** greet someone, answer “How are you?”, and close a very short exchange.

## 1. Recall — 5 minutes

Repeat the three Day 1 frames once: “My name is…”, “I'm from…”, “I live in…”. Looking at the model is allowed.

## 2. Learn four useful chunks — 10–15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Hi. / Hello. | Hola. |
| How are you? | ¿Cómo estás? |
| I'm fine, thanks. | Estoy bien, gracias. |
| And you? | ¿Y tú? |
| Goodbye. / Bye. | Adiós. |

Listen and repeat each chunk twice. Do not study extra variations yet.

## 3. Guided listening — 10 minutes

Listen twice without reading:

> A: Hi!  
> B: Hello!  
> A: How are you?  
> B: I'm fine, thanks. And you?  
> A: I'm fine, thanks. Bye!  
> B: Goodbye!

Answer:
1. What greeting do you hear?
2. How does B answer?
3. What do they say at the end?

Answer with a word or short phrase. Reveal the script only after listening. If audio was not played, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Build the exchange — 10–15 minutes

First repeat the model with the text. Then complete only the missing part:
- A: Hi!
- B: ______!
- A: How are you?
- B: I'm ______, thanks.
- A: Bye!
- B: ______!

Now practise the full exchange once. You may read the lines; fluency without the script is not expected yet.

## 5. Check and record — 5 minutes

Can you say “Hi”, “How are you?”, “I'm fine, thanks” and “Bye” with understandable pronunciation? Mark *Ready for review* or *Review*. Record whether you listened to audio and which phrase needs another repetition.

**Completion rule:** do not mark Approved without the actual practice and evidence. Anki is optional, not a required task.
