# Day 048 — Disculparse y aclarar malentendidos

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** apologise politely, explain a misunderstanding, and check that both people understand each other.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Apologies and clarification — 15 minutes

| English | Meaning in Spanish | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **I'm sorry about that.** | Lo siento por eso. | Acknowledge a problem or inconvenience. |
| **I misunderstood.** | Entendí mal. | Admit that you understood something incorrectly. |
| **What I meant was…** | Lo que quise decir fue… | Explain your intended meaning. |
| **Let me explain.** | Déjame explicarlo. | Offer a clearer explanation. |
| **Could you explain what you mean?** | ¿Podría explicar qué quiere decir? | Ask the other person to clarify. |
| **So, you mean…?** | Entonces, ¿quiere decir que…? | Check your understanding. |
| **I see what you mean.** | Entiendo lo que quiere decir. | Show that you understand now. |
| **Thanks for explaining.** | Gracias por explicarlo. | Close the clarification politely. |

**Practice**
1. Match each English phrase to its Spanish meaning.
2. Complete aloud: “I'm sorry, I misunderstood. What I meant was…”
3. Create two short examples. Use real information or label the scenario as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the text first. If studying alone, cover the script while listening.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Ana: Are we meeting at half past six?  
> Ben: Sorry, I thought you said seven.  
> Ana: Oh, I see. I meant six thirty because the film starts at seven.  
> Ben: I'm sorry, I misunderstood. I can be there at six thirty.  
> Ana: Great. So we're meeting outside the cinema at six thirty, right?  
> Ben: Yes, that's right. Thanks for checking.

**Questions**
1. What time did Ben think they were meeting?
2. Why does Ana want to meet at six thirty?
3. What misunderstanding happened?
4. How do they confirm the final plan?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading the script is not listening evidence. After answering, shadow two lines. Notice the difference between **I thought you said…** and **I meant…**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Complete two role-plays. Use keywords, not a full script.

**Role-play A — Wrong time:** You and a friend understood different meeting times. Apologise, explain what you meant, and confirm the correct time.

**Role-play B — Wrong instruction:** You misunderstood a simple instruction at a hotel, shop, or work. Say what you understood, ask for clarification, and repeat the correct instruction in your own words.

Use at least three phrases from Section 2. If an AI tutor is available, ask it to play the other person and give corrections in Spanish after your response. Retry one corrected exchange.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at notes:
- Recall at least five phrases and their meanings.
- Complete one role-play with an apology and clarification.
- Answer at least three of the four listening questions correctly.
- Confirm the corrected information in your own words.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 using the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable role-play. If you cannot explain the misunderstanding or confirm the correction, mark **Review** and retry.
