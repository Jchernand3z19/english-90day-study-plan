# Daily Lessons

Each lesson should include a clear objective, a short Spanish explanation, useful English examples, listening or shadowing practice, spoken production, and a self-check.

## Standard lesson format

1. **Goal:** what you should be able to say or understand.
2. **Warm-up:** retrieve previously learned phrases.
3. **Language focus:** vocabulary and one useful structure.
4. **Listen and repeat:** use a short, level-appropriate audio source; record the source and duration in the daily log.
5. **Speak:** answer prompts aloud without reading a full script.
6. **Check:** complete the lesson questions without looking at the answer key.
7. **Evidence:** record actual results in daily-logs.

Use accurate, natural examples and explain grammar in Spanish when it helps. Avoid introducing too many new phrases at once. Do not claim an external audio resource was checked unless it was actually opened and reviewed.
