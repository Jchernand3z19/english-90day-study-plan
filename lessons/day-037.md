## 1. Quick retrieval — 5–10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Travel phrase review — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, say a phrase for each purpose:

| Purpose | English | Español |
|---|---|---|
| Ask for directions | How do I get to the station? | ¿Cómo llego a la estación? |
| Confirm a time | What time does boarding start? | ¿A qué hora empieza el embarque? |
| Ask for help | Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |
| Confirm a reservation | I'd like to confirm my booking. | Quisiera confirmar mi reserva. |
| Ask someone to repeat | Could you repeat that, please? | ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor? |

Choose three phrases and adapt them to a fictional travel situation. Say each one twice without reading.

## 3. Mini role-play — 5–10 minutes

Role-play asking for directions or confirming a booking. Ask two questions and repeat one key detail to check understanding. If a tutor or AI is available, ask for one correction in Spanish and retry the corrected phrase.

## 4. Next target — 2 minutes

Record one phrase you remembered easily and one travel skill to review. No score is required today.
