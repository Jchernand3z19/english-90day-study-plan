# Day 028 — Repaso ligero / descanso

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** refresh familiar everyday phrases without adding heavy new material, and notice what still needs review before the Phase 1 assessment.

This is intentionally lighter than a normal lesson. Rest is allowed. If you take a full rest day, leave this lesson pending; do not count rest as completed practice.

## 1. Quick retrieval — 5–8 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Everyday phrase review — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, try to say one phrase for each situation:

| Situation | Example phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Asking for help | Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |
| Asking someone to repeat | Could you repeat that, please? | ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor? |
| Ordering or requesting | I'd like a coffee, please. | Quisiera un café, por favor. |
| Asking for directions | How do I get to the bus station? | ¿Cómo llego a la estación de autobuses? |
| Describing a routine | I usually start work at eight. | Normalmente empiezo a trabajar a las ocho. |

Choose three phrases and adapt them to your own life or to a clearly labelled role-play. Say each one twice without reading.

## 3. Mini speaking review — 5–8 minutes

Speak for 30–60 seconds about your typical day. Then role-play one short situation: ordering something, asking for help, or asking for directions. Aim to communicate, not to speak perfectly.

If a tutor or AI is available, ask for only one or two corrections in Spanish. Repeat the corrected sentence once.

## 4. Check and plan — 2 minutes

Write down:
- one phrase you remembered easily;
- one phrase you forgot or said incorrectly;
- one skill to practise before the Phase 1 assessment.

No score is required for this light review. Use the result to guide practice, not to claim that the phase has been passed.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, phrases practised, and one next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, and **Approved** only after completing this light review. A full rest day remains pending.
