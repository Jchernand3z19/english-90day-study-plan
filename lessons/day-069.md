# Day 069 — Common phrasal verbs

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** understand and use common phrasal verbs in everyday conversation without translating each word separately.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| Phrasal verb | Español | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **find out** | averiguar | I need to find out what time the train leaves. |
| **look for** | buscar | I'm looking for my keys. |
| **pick up** | recoger | Can you pick me up at six? |
| **get along with** | llevarse bien con | I get along with my coworkers. |
| **run out of** | quedarse sin | We ran out of coffee. |
| **put off** | posponer | We put off the meeting until Friday. |

Read all six phrases aloud and cover the Spanish meanings. Actively practise three core phrasal verbs today: **find out**, **look for**, and **pick up**. For **get along with**, **run out of**, and **put off**, focus first on recognising the meaning in the dialogue. Make one original sentence for each core phrase; label invented details as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Leo: Have you found out what time the bus leaves?
> Sara: Not yet. I'm looking for the schedule online.
> Leo: I can pick you up if the bus is too late.
> Sara: Thanks. I get along well with my neighbour, so I might ask him too.
> Leo: Good idea. Just don't put off deciding until the last minute.
> Sara: You're right. I don't want to run out of time.

**Comprehension questions**
1. What information is Leo asking about?
2. What is Sara looking for?
3. What offer does Leo make?
4. What does Sara want to avoid running out of?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then shadow two lines and repeat them with natural rhythm. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening as **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Make one original spoken sentence with each of the three core phrasal verbs: **find out**, **look for**, and **pick up**. Then role-play a practical situation using at least two of them: looking for a place, arranging a pickup, or finding out information. Finally, identify the meaning of **get along with**, **run out of**, and **put off** in the dialogue. Explain the meanings in Spanish if needed.

Use keywords, not a full script. Ask for feedback in Spanish after your response, then repeat one corrected answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Recall the meanings of **find out**, **look for**, and **pick up**.
- Use all three core phrasal verbs in original spoken sentences.
- Recognise the meaning of at least two of the other three phrasal verbs.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.
- Do not treat a phrasal verb as a literal word-for-word translation.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt when assessed and an understandable oral response. If the core task is not yet possible without reading a complete script, mark **Review**.
