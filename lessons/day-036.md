# Day 036 — Role-play de reserva y llegada

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** check in to a hotel, explain a reservation, and respond to a simple issue politely.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **I have a reservation under the name…** | Tengo una reserva a nombre de… |
| **Could I check in, please?** | ¿Podría registrarme, por favor? |
| **Is breakfast included?** | ¿El desayuno está incluido? |
| **What time is check-out?** | ¿A qué hora es la salida? |
| **The room key doesn't seem to work.** | La llave de la habitación parece no funcionar. |
| **Could you check that for me?** | ¿Podría comprobarlo por mí? |

Say each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish meaning and try to retrieve it. Make three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Guest: Hello. I have a reservation under the name Taylor.
> Receptionist: Welcome. Could I see your ID, please?
> Guest: Of course. Is breakfast included?
> Receptionist: Yes, breakfast is from seven to ten. Check-out is at eleven.
> Guest: Thank you. I think the room key doesn't work.
> Receptionist: I'm sorry. I'll make you a new key.
> Guest: Thank you for your help.

**Questions**
1. Under what name is the reservation?
2. What time is breakfast?
3. What time is check-out?
4. What problem does the guest report?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Complete a hotel check-in role-play. Confirm the reservation name, ask about breakfast and check-out, then explain a problem with the key or room. Ask for help politely and respond to the receptionist.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Complete the check-in sequence.
- Ask two relevant questions.
- Explain one problem politely.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable oral production. If the core task still requires reading a full script, mark **Review**.
