# Day 080 — Five-minute professional conversation

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** maintain a short professional conversation by introducing yourself, exchanging relevant information, asking follow-up questions, and closing politely.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Conversation stages — 15 minutes

| Stage | Useful English | Spanish meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Open | **Hello, it's nice to meet you.** | Hola, mucho gusto. |
| Ask about work | **What kind of work do you do?** | ¿A qué tipo de trabajo se dedica? |
| Add detail | **My main responsibilities include…** | Mis principales responsabilidades incluyen… |
| Show interest | **That sounds interesting. How does it work?** | Suena interesante. ¿Cómo funciona? |
| Share a goal | **I'm hoping to learn more about…** | Espero aprender más sobre… |
| Follow up | **How did you get started in that field?** | ¿Cómo empezó en ese campo? |
| Close | **It was nice speaking with you.** | Fue un gusto conversar con usted. |

Do not memorise a complete dialogue. Learn the stages and choose phrases as needed. Use truthful details about your work or interests.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the exchange twice without showing you the script first.

> Rosa: Hello, I'm Rosa. What kind of work do you do?  
> David: I work with reports and business information. How about you?  
> Rosa: I coordinate projects for a small team. What do you enjoy most about your work?  
> David: I enjoy solving problems and making information easier to understand.  
> Rosa: That sounds interesting. How did you get started?  
> David: I learned by doing projects and asking colleagues for feedback.  
> Rosa: It was nice speaking with you.  
> David: You too. Have a great day.

Questions:
1. What kind of work does David do?
2. What does he enjoy most?
3. How did he get started?
4. How does Rosa close the conversation?

Check after answering, then shadow two lines.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Complete a five-minute professional conversation with a tutor, AI partner, or study partner. Include:
1. a greeting and introduction;
2. one question about the other person's work;
3. one relevant follow-up question;
4. one short detail about your own experience or interests;
5. a polite closing.

If practising alone, record a two-sided role-play, pausing between turns. Do not claim it was a live conversation. Use keywords rather than a full script. Ask for corrections in Spanish and repeat one improved exchange.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use at least four phrases from Section 2.
- Ask two relevant questions.
- Respond to a follow-up without reading a full script.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric. If you only deliver an introduction without responding to the other person, mark **Review** and practise follow-up questions.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, phrase retrieval, listening method, conversation format (live or solo role-play), score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started and **Approved** only when the evidence meets the rubric.
