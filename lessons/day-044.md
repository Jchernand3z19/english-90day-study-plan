# Day 044 — Repaso ligero / descanso

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** refresh past-tense storytelling and questions about experiences before moving to problem-solving language.

This is intentionally lighter than a normal lesson. A full rest day is fine; if you rest, leave the lesson pending.

## 1. Quick retrieval — 5–10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say the phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Storytelling phrase review — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, say a phrase for each purpose:

| Purpose | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Start a story | You won't believe what happened. | No vas a creer lo que pasó. |
| Give the first event | At first,… | Al principio… |
| Add the next event | Then,… / After that,… | Luego… / Después de eso… |
| Describe a surprise | Suddenly,… | De repente… |
| Explain the outcome | In the end,… | Al final… |
| Ask about someone else's experience | What was it like? | ¿Cómo fue? |

Choose three phrases and use them to tell a short story aloud.

## 3. Mini speaking — 5–10 minutes

Speak for 45–60 seconds about a real experience or a clearly labelled fictional event. Include a beginning, two events, and an ending. Then ask or answer one follow-up question about the story.

If a tutor or AI is available, request one correction in Spanish and repeat the corrected sentence.

## 4. Choose your next target — 2 minutes

Record one storytelling skill that feels reliable and one that needs practice. If you have not yet used past-tense verbs reliably, review Days 38–43 before moving on.
