# Day 086 — Repairing pauses and misunderstandings

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** keep a conversation going when you need time, do not understand, or need to correct a misunderstanding.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Let me think for a moment.** | Déjeme pensar un momento. |
| **Could you repeat the last part?** | ¿Podría repetir la última parte? |
| **Could you rephrase that?** | ¿Podría decirlo de otra manera? |
| **If I understand correctly,…** | Si entiendo bien… |
| **Sorry, I meant…** | Perdón, quise decir… |
| **Thanks for your patience.** | Gracias por su paciencia. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Interviewer: Could you explain why you changed the plan?
>
> Candidate: Let me think for a moment. We changed it because the original deadline was too short.
>
> Interviewer: Do you mean the team needed more time?
>
> Candidate: Yes, that's right. Sorry, I meant that we needed more time to check the information carefully.
>
> Interviewer: I understand.
>
> Candidate: Thanks for your patience.

**Questions**
1. What question does the interviewer ask?
2. Why was the plan changed?
3. What does the candidate clarify?
4. Which phrase does the candidate use to gain time?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play a conversation where you do not understand a question or accidentally say the wrong thing. Use one pause phrase, one clarification phrase, and one correction. Continue the conversation instead of stopping after the mistake.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use a phrase to gain time or ask for clarification.
- Correct or confirm one misunderstanding.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
