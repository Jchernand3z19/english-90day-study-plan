# Day 018 — Imperatives for directions

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** give and follow short instructions using imperatives and location clues.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it and record only the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Walk to the end of the street. | Camine hasta el final de la calle. |
| Turn right. | Gire a la derecha. |
| Cross the road. | Cruce la calle. |
| Take the second street on your left. | Tome la segunda calle a la izquierda. |
| Go past the bank. | Pase el banco. |
| The museum is opposite the park. | El museo está frente al parque. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and use them in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Guide: Walk to the end of this street.  
Visitor: Okay. Then what should I do?  
Guide: Turn right, cross the road, and take the second street on your left.  
Visitor: Should I cross at the traffic lights?  
Guide: Yes, please. The museum is opposite the park.  
Visitor: Thank you. I'll go straight, turn right, cross at the lights, and take the second left.

**Questions**
1. Where should the visitor walk first?  
2. Which direction should the visitor turn?  
3. Where should the visitor cross?  
4. What is opposite the museum?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Give someone a four-step route to a familiar place. Check understanding by asking “Could you repeat the directions?”

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
