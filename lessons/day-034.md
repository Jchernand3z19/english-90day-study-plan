# Day 034 — Fechas, horarios y confirmaciones

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** confirm dates and times clearly and check important details to avoid misunderstandings.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **I'd like to confirm my booking.** | Quisiera confirmar mi reserva. |
| **That's for Friday, October 30th, right?** | Es para el viernes 30 de octubre, ¿correcto? |
| **What time should I arrive?** | ¿A qué hora debo llegar? |
| **Could you repeat the date, please?** | ¿Podría repetir la fecha, por favor? |
| **So, that's at half past six.** | Entonces, es a las seis y media. |
| **Could you send me a confirmation?** | ¿Podría enviarme una confirmación? |

Say each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish meaning and try to retrieve it. Make three original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

*All dates and booking details in this dialogue are fictional role-play examples, not real reservations.*

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

******Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Agent: Good morning. How can I help?
> Guest: I'd like to confirm my booking for Friday, October 30th.
> Agent: Yes, it's for one room, arriving at 6:30 p.m.
> Guest: Could you repeat the arrival time, please?
> Agent: Half past six in the evening.
> Guest: Thank you. Could you send me a confirmation by email?
> Agent: Certainly. I'll send it today.

**Questions**
1. What date is the booking for?
2. What time will the guest arrive?
3. What does the guest ask the agent to repeat?
4. How will the confirmation be sent?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Practise confirming a fictional hotel, restaurant, or transport booking. Say the date slowly, confirm the time, repeat one key detail, and ask for written confirmation. Use a fictional booking, not a claim about a real reservation.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- State a date and time clearly.
- Ask someone to repeat a detail.
- Repeat the confirmed information accurately.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable oral production. If the core task still requires reading a full script, mark **Review**.
