# Day 001 — A very short personal introduction

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** understand and say three personal facts using sentence models. This is a lesson, not a repeat of the initial diagnostic.

**Baseline note:** the separate written diagnostic, British Council A1 listening test, and spoken-presentation sample are already recorded. Do not repeat them. The earlier improvised voice exercise was not this lesson and does not count as completion.

## 1. Start gently — 5 minutes

Say any English words you already know. One-word answers are acceptable. Do not force complete sentences yet.

## 2. Learn three sentence frames — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| My name is… | Me llamo… |
| I'm from Honduras. | Soy de Honduras. |
| I live in Choloma. | Vivo en Choloma. |
| I'm learning English. | Estoy aprendiendo inglés. |
| I like music. | Me gusta la música. |

Listen to each model, repeat it slowly, then repeat it once at a natural pace. Replace only the information after the model; keep the rest of the sentence.

**Build one sentence at a time**
- My name is [your name].
- I'm from [country].
- I live in [city].

Do not add extra details until these three are comfortable.

## 3. Guided listening — 10–15 minutes

Ask the tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the following three sentences twice. Hide the text while listening.

> Hello. My name is Alex. I'm from Canada. I live in Toronto.

Answer with a word or short phrase:
1. What is the name?
2. Which country?
3. Which city?

Listen again and repeat each sentence after the speaker. Reveal the text only after the two listens. If you did not hear audio, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Supported speaking — 10–15 minutes

Complete these frames aloud. You may look at the model:
- My name is ______.
- I'm from ______.
- I live in ______.

Then say the three sentences together. If you get stuck, return to one sentence; do not improvise a longer speech.

## 5. Check — 5 minutes

Without looking, try to say:
1. Me llamo…
2. Soy de Honduras.
3. Vivo en…

**Suggested result:** mark *Ready for review* only if you can say at least two of the three sentence frames understandably with the model removed. Otherwise mark *Review*. Listening remains *Not assessed* if no audio was heard. This is not a phase-level assessment.

## Evidence

Record which sentence frames you could say independently, which needed the model, whether audio was actually heard, and one item to practise next time.

## Completion rule

Keep Day 1 **Pending** until the activities are actually completed and logged. Do not treat the initial diagnostic or the earlier improvised exercise as completion.
