# Day 001 — Diagnostic and Personal Introduction

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Main objective:** introduce yourself in simple English and identify the areas that need the most practice.

## 1. Warm-up — 10 minutes

Review 5–10 familiar Anki phrases. Before revealing each answer, try to say the English phrase aloud. Record how many you recalled without looking in your daily log.

Do not worry if you remember very little. This is a baseline, not a test you need to pass.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

Study these sentence patterns:

| English | Meaning in Spanish |
|---|---|
| Hi, my name is… | Hola, me llamo… |
| I'm from Honduras. | Soy de Honduras. |
| I live in San Pedro Sula. | Vivo en San Pedro Sula. |
| I work in data analysis. | Trabajo en análisis de datos. |
| I work with reports and dashboards. | Trabajo con informes y paneles de datos. |
| In my free time, I like… | En mi tiempo libre, me gusta… |
| I'm learning English because… | Estoy aprendiendo inglés porque… |
| Could you speak more slowly, please? | ¿Podría hablar más despacio, por favor? |
| Could you repeat that, please? | ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor? |
| I don't understand yet. | Todavía no entiendo. |

### One useful structure

Use **I am / I'm** for identity or description:

- I'm Juan.
- I'm from Honduras.
- I'm interested in learning English.

Use **I work** to describe your work:

- I work in data analysis.
- I work with reports.

Use **I like + noun or verb ending in -ing**:

- I like music.
- I like learning new things.

You do not need to memorize every rule today. Focus on saying the examples naturally.

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Use the English examples above as your first practice material if you do not have an audio source available.

1. Read one sentence slowly.
2. Listen to a pronunciation source if available, or use a text-to-speech tool you already trust.
3. Repeat the sentence three times.
4. Repeat once without looking.
5. Mark the sentences that are difficult to pronounce.

Do not write in the log that you listened to external audio unless you actually used an audio source.

## 4. Spoken production — 15 minutes

Answer aloud. You may write keywords, but do not read a complete script while speaking.

1. What's your name?
2. Where are you from?
3. Where do you live?
4. What do you do?
5. What do you like doing in your free time?
6. Why are you learning English?
7. What do you say when you don't understand someone?

Then give a short introduction of 30–45 seconds. If it is difficult, use the sentence patterns and try again with less reading.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at the table, try to produce the English for these meanings:

1. Me llamo…
2. Soy de Honduras.
3. Trabajo en análisis de datos.
4. En mi tiempo libre, me gusta…
5. ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor?
6. Estoy aprendiendo inglés porque…

**Suggested pass for Day 1:** produce at least 4 of the 6 target meanings understandably and complete the spoken introduction. If you do not meet this yet, mark the session **Revisión** and practice the missing phrases; this is useful diagnostic information.

## Evidence to record

Create a daily log using the template in daily-logs/README.md and record:

- Whether you completed each part.
- How many target phrases you produced without looking.
- The introduction you actually managed to say, or a short description of it.
- Three phrases that were difficult.
- The result of the self-check.
- One specific goal for Day 2.
