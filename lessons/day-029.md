# Day 029 — Evaluación integrada de fase 1

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** demonstrate the everyday English skills practised in Phase 1 and identify what needs review.

This checkpoint uses the official task weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md`. It is not an official CEFR exam. Do not mark the phase as passed just because you reached Day 29.

## 1. Prepare — 5 minutes

Look over your Phase 1 notes for five minutes. Then close them. Do not rehearse complete scripts. You may ask for instructions in Spanish, but attempt the tasks in English.

## 2. Task A — Personal introduction (10 minutes, 20 points)

Speak for 45–60 seconds. Include:
- your name and where you are from;
- where you live;
- your work or a typical daily activity;
- one interest;
- why you are learning English.

Use simple sentences. If you make a mistake, continue rather than restarting the whole introduction.

## 3. Task B — Everyday role-play (15 minutes, 25 points)

Choose one scenario:
- buy an item in a shop;
- order food and a drink;
- ask for a service.

Ask at least two questions and make one polite request. Example phrases: **How much is this?**, **I'd like a coffee, please**, **Could you help me?**, **Can I pay by card?** The other speaker should respond naturally and ask one follow-up question.

## 4. Task C — Directions and routine (10 minutes, 20 points)

First, ask how to get to a place and explain a simple route using words such as **turn left**, **turn right**, **go straight**, and **next to**. Then describe three parts of a typical day using the present simple, such as **I start work at…** and **I usually…**.

## 5. Task D — Listening (10 minutes, 20 points)

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read a beginner-level dialogue twice without showing you the text. Answer:
1. What is the conversation mainly about?
2. Where does the person want to go or what does the person want?
3. What time, price, or other key detail is mentioned?

If you do not have a way to listen to a script or audio, mark this task **Not assessed**; do not substitute reading for listening.

## 6. Task E — Retrieval and clarification (5 minutes, 15 points)

Without looking at notes, say five useful phrases from Phase 1. Then use one clarification phrase naturally, such as **Could you repeat that, please?** or **Could you speak more slowly, please?**

## Scoring and next step

Use the fixed weights in `assessments/phase-assessments.md` (20 + 25 + 20 + 20 + 15 = 100). Record actual task scores and evidence. Suggested pass: at least 80/100, with all tasks attempted and the main message understandable. If listening was not assessed or any task was not attempted, mark the result **Incomplete**, not Passed.

If below 80, mark **Review**. Choose the two weakest skills and use Day 30 to practise them. Day 30 is not an automatic pass.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, task responses, listening method, score by task, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep this lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the assessment rules.
