# Day 021 — Week 3 review

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** refresh location, directions, and transport language and identify one skill to practise again.

This is a light review day, not an automatic pass. A full rest day is allowed; if you rest, leave the lesson pending.

## 1. Quick retrieval — 5 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Recall directions and transport — 10 minutes

Without looking at notes, say a phrase for each purpose:

| Purpose | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Ask for directions | How do I get to the bus station? | ¿Cómo llego a la estación de autobuses? |
| Give an instruction | Go straight and turn left. | Siga recto y gire a la izquierda. |
| Ask about distance | Is it far from here? | ¿Está lejos de aquí? |
| Ask about a bus | Does this bus go downtown? | ¿Este autobús va al centro? |
| Confirm a location | Is it next to the bank? | ¿Está al lado del banco? |

Choose three phrases and say them in a short directions role-play without reading.

## 3. Mini speaking — 5–10 minutes

Ask for directions to a place, then repeat the route in your own words. Include two direction words and one location phrase. If a tutor or AI is available, request one correction in Spanish and retry.

## 4. Choose one target — 2 minutes

Record one phrase you remembered and one that still needs practice. No score is required today.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki cards reviewed, phrases practised, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; a full rest day remains pending and does not count as completed practice.
