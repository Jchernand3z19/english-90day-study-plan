# Day 060 — Corrección de errores y cierre de fase 2

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use evidence from Day 59 to correct recurring errors and decide honestly whether Phase 2 skills need more practice.

This is a review day, not an automatic pass. If Day 59 was not assessed, first complete or schedule the missing assessment tasks.

## 1. Choose your priorities — 5 minutes

Review the Day 59 results. Select the two weakest areas: travel requests, past-tense storytelling, explaining a problem, social conversation, or listening and clarification. If you have no recorded results, mark them **Not assessed** rather than guessing.

## 2. Correct three real errors — 15 minutes

Choose up to three corrections from your own work. For each one, record:

- **What I said or did:** the original phrase or error.
- **Better version:** the corrected English.
- **Why:** a short explanation in Spanish.
- **Retry:** say the corrected phrase aloud twice, then use it in a new sentence.

Do not invent errors just to fill the table. If you have no recorded corrections, ask a tutor to give you three short prompts and record the real feedback.

## 3. Targeted listening — 10–15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing it first. Answer the questions aloud.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Receptionist: Good afternoon. How can I help you?  
> Guest: I booked a room for last night, but the hotel couldn't find my reservation.  
> Receptionist: I'm sorry about that. Did you book online?  
> Guest: Yes, I did. I have the confirmation email here.  
> Receptionist: Thank you. Let me check the system.  
> Guest: Could you tell me if a room is available tonight?  
> Receptionist: Yes, we have one room available. I can help you make a new reservation.

**Questions**
1. What problem did the guest have?
2. What evidence does the guest have?
3. What does the guest ask about?
4. What solution does the receptionist offer?

After answering, shadow two lines. If listening is unavailable, record **Not assessed**; do not replace it with reading aloud.

## 4. Retry your weakest speaking task — 10–15 minutes

Repeat one task from Day 59, choosing the skill that needs the most work:
- retell a short past event with **first**, **then**, and **finally**;
- explain a travel problem and ask for a solution;
- role-play confirming a reservation;
- maintain a conversation and ask follow-up questions.

Use keywords, not a full script. Compare this attempt with your first attempt and note one improvement and one remaining difficulty.

## 5. Phase 2 decision — 5–10 minutes

Use the fixed 100-point scoring system in `assessments/phase-assessments.md`.

- **Ready to progress:** at least 80/100, all tasks assessed, and the main message understandable.
- **Review:** below 80/100 or one or more essential skills still unreliable.
- **Incomplete:** evidence or an assessment task is missing.

If you are in Review or Incomplete, keep the relevant skills active while beginning new material only when the prerequisites are adequate. Record the exact skill to revisit; do not automatically mark Phase 2 as passed.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, errors corrected, listening method and answers, retry task, decision, and next review target. Keep this lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when the review evidence is complete.
