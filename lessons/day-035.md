# Day 035 — Preguntas útiles para viajar

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask for essential travel information and confirm directions, times, and costs.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **How do I get to the train station?** | ¿Cómo llego a la estación de tren? |
| **How far is it?** | ¿Qué tan lejos está? |
| **How long does it take?** | ¿Cuánto tiempo tarda? |
| **Which platform does it leave from?** | ¿De qué andén sale? |
| **How much is a ticket?** | ¿Cuánto cuesta un billete? |
| **Could you show me on the map?** | ¿Podría mostrarme en el mapa? |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

******Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Traveller: Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
> Local: Go straight for two blocks and turn right.
> Traveller: How far is it?
> Local: About a ten-minute walk.
> Traveller: Thank you. Do you know which platform the train to the city leaves from?
> Local: Platform three, but check the board for updates.

**Questions**
1. Which direction should the traveller go?
2. How long is the walk?
3. Which platform is mentioned?
4. What should the traveller check for updates?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Role-play asking a local or station worker for directions, travel time, and a platform or ticket price. Repeat the key information to confirm you understood.

Use keywords rather than a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask two relevant travel questions.
- Confirm a direction and one detail.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review** and practise the weak skill again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
