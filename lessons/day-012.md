# Day 012 — Asking for help with a service

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask staff for help, identify what you do not understand, and request an explanation.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Could you help me with this? | ¿Podría ayudarme con esto? |
| I don't understand this question. | No entiendo esta pregunta. |
| Could you explain it, please? | ¿Podría explicarlo, por favor? |
| Could you repeat the last part? | ¿Podría repetir la última parte? |
| Which part is difficult? | ¿Qué parte es difícil? |
| I see. Thank you. | Ya entiendo. Gracias. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and try to retrieve the meaning. Choose one phrase and repeat it with a different object or question.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Customer: Excuse me, could you help me with this form?  
Staff: Of course. Which part is difficult?  
Customer: I don't understand this question. Could you explain it, please?  
Staff: It asks for your home address.  
Customer: I see. Could you repeat the last part?  
Staff: Your home address.

**Questions**
1. What does the customer need help with?  
2. Which part is unclear?  
3. What information does the question ask for?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Use one simple situation: point to a question on a form and say “I don't understand this question. Could you explain it, please?” Read the model first, then repeat only the request. No extra explanation is required.

Ask for one or two corrections in Spanish if a tutor is available, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
