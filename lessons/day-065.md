# Day 065 — Discussing everyday topics

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** sustain a short conversation about familiar topics by adding details, reacting naturally, and asking follow-up questions.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use each in a new sentence. Record actual counts.

## 2. Conversation building blocks — 15 minutes

| English | Meaning in Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **How about you?** | ¿Y tú? / ¿Y usted? | I like walking after work. How about you? |
| **Really? What was it like?** | ¿En serio? ¿Cómo fue? | I visited a new café. Really? What was it like? |
| **That sounds interesting.** | Eso suena interesante. | I started learning photography. That sounds interesting. |
| **What do you like about it?** | ¿Qué te gusta de eso? | What do you like about your job? |
| **I haven't tried it yet.** | Todavía no lo he probado. | I haven't tried that restaurant yet. |
| **For example…** | Por ejemplo… | I enjoy outdoor activities, for example, hiking. |

**Practice:** Pick one topic—food, music, films, work, exercise, or travel. Give two details about your opinion and prepare two follow-up questions.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

> Sam: What do you like doing after work?  
> Rita: I usually go for a walk or watch a series. How about you?  
> Sam: I like cooking. I tried making Thai food last weekend.  
> Rita: Really? What was it like?  
> Sam: It was difficult, but fun. I want to try again.  
> Rita: That sounds interesting. What do you like about cooking?  
> Sam: I like learning new things, and I can share the food with my family.

Answer aloud:
1. What does Rita usually do after work?
2. What did Sam try last weekend?
3. How did Sam feel about it?
4. Why does Sam like cooking?

Check after answering and shadow two lines.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Have a 3-minute conversation with a tutor, AI partner, or study partner about your chosen topic. If you practise alone, record yourself answering:
1. What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
2. Why do you enjoy it?
3. When did you last do it?
4. What would you recommend to someone else?

Ask at least three follow-up questions during a live role-play. Use natural reactions such as **Really?**, **That sounds fun**, or **Why do you like it?** Repeat the conversation once with fewer notes.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Speak for at least one minute without reading a full script.
- Give a reason and one example.
- Ask or produce three relevant follow-up questions.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric. If the conversation stops after one-word answers, mark **Review** and practise adding a reason or example.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki review, listening method, topic, follow-up questions, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started and **Approved** only when the evidence meets the rubric.
