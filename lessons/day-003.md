# Day 003 — Basic Questions to Get to Know Someone

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** ask and answer simple questions about a person.

## 1. Anki warm-up — 10 minutes

Review previous phrases. Say the English answer before checking it. Revisit any greeting phrase that still feels difficult.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| Question | Meaning | Example answer |
|---|---|---|
| What's your name? | ¿Cómo te llamas? | My name is Juan. |
| Where are you from? | ¿De dónde eres? | I'm from Honduras. |
| Where do you live? | ¿Dónde vives? | I live in San Pedro Sula. |
| What do you do? | ¿A qué te dedicas? | I work in data analysis. |
| What do you like to do? | ¿Qué te gusta hacer? | I like listening to music. |
| How was your day? | ¿Cómo estuvo tu día? | It was good, thanks. |
| Do you speak English? | ¿Hablas inglés? | A little. I'm learning. |

### A simple question pattern

Many questions use a question word plus a helping verb:

- Where **do** you live?
- What **do** you do?
- What **do** you like?

For questions with “be,” use the verb directly:

- Where **are** you from?
- How **are** you?

Do not try to learn every grammar rule today. Practice the whole question as a phrase.

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Read the exchange, then repeat both roles.

A: Hi! What's your name?  
B: My name is Juan. What's your name?  
A: I'm Alex. Nice to meet you.  
B: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?  
A: I'm from Canada. How about you?  
B: I'm from Honduras.

Try to reduce the pauses while keeping the words clear.

## 4. Speak — 15 minutes

Answer all seven questions aloud. Then ask the same questions as if you were talking to a new person. Add one follow-up question, such as “What do you like to do in your free time?”

Do not invent personal facts. Use your own true answers or clearly label a role-play character as fictional.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking, produce:

1. ¿De dónde eres?
2. ¿Dónde vives?
3. ¿A qué te dedicas?
4. Hablo un poco. Estoy aprendiendo.
5. ¿Qué te gusta hacer?

**Suggested pass:** produce at least 4 of 5 meanings understandably and complete a short exchange with at least three questions.

## Evidence

Record the score, questions that were difficult, and two answers you can now say without reading. If you need the table to answer, mark the lesson as in progress or review rather than approved.
