## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the exchange twice without showing it first. Answer the questions, then check the text and repeat both roles.

A: Hi! What's your name?  
B: My name is Juan. What's your name?  
A: I'm Alex. Nice to meet you.  
B: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?  
A: I'm from Canada. How about you?  
B: I'm from Honduras.

**Questions**
1. What is Speaker B's name?
2. Where is Alex from?
3. Where is Speaker B from?

If you did not actually hear the exchange, mark listening **Not assessed**. Try to reduce pauses while keeping the words clear.
