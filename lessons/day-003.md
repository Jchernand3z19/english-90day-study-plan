# Day 003 — Ask and say a name

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 40–50 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask another person's name and answer with a short model.

## 1. Recall — 5 minutes

Say “Hi”, “How are you?” and “I'm fine, thanks.” Use the notes if needed.

## 2. Learn the target chunks — 10–15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| What's your name? | ¿Cómo te llamas? |
| My name is… | Me llamo… |
| I'm… | Soy… / Me llamo… |
| Nice to meet you. | Mucho gusto. |
| Nice to meet you too. | Igualmente. |

Repeat each phrase after hearing it. Practise the question and answer as a pair.

## 3. Guided listening — 10 minutes

Listen twice before looking at the script:

> A: Hi. What's your name?  
> B: I'm Alex.  
> A: I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.  
> B: Nice to meet you too.

Answer:
1. Is the first speaker Alex or Sam?
2. What is the second speaker's name?
3. What do they say after introducing themselves?

Reveal the script after listening. If audio was not played, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Supported speaking — 10–15 minutes

Use the exact model:
- A: Hi. What's your name?
- B: My name is ______.
- A: Nice to meet you.
- B: Nice to meet you too.

Switch roles once. You may read the dialogue. Then try only the answer “My name is…” without looking.

## 5. Check — 5 minutes

Say the question and answer once. If you can communicate both with the model available, mark *Ready for review*. If not, mark *Review*. Record which line was difficult.

**Completion rule:** no requirement to speak for 20–30 seconds or invent additional questions today.
