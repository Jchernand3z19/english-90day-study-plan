# Day 016 — Asking for and giving directions

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask how to reach a place and understand a short route with turns and landmarks.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| How do I get to the bus station? | ¿Cómo llego a la estación de autobuses? |
| Go straight for two blocks. | Siga recto dos cuadras. |
| Turn left/right. | Gire a la izquierda/derecha. |
| At the traffic lights… | En el semáforo… |
| Is it far from here? | ¿Está lejos de aquí? |
| It's about a five-minute walk. | Está a unos cinco minutos caminando. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose one direction phrase and repeat it with a different turn.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Visitor: Excuse me, how do I get to the bus station?  
Local: Go straight for two blocks and turn left at the traffic lights.  
Visitor: Is it far from here?  
Local: No, it's about a five-minute walk.  
Visitor: Thank you. Is it next to the shopping centre?  
Local: Yes, it's opposite the main entrance.

**Questions**
1. Where does the visitor want to go?  
2. How far should the visitor walk before turning?  
3. Which direction should the visitor turn?  
4. Where is the bus station in relation to the shopping centre?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Start with two instructions: “Go straight” and “Turn left.” Point to the route or use a simple map. Add one landmark only after those two instructions are clear. Repeating a full route is optional.

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
