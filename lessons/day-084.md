# Day 084 — Summarizing and paraphrasing

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** summarise a short message in your own words while preserving the main idea and key details.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |\n|---|---|\n| **The main point is…** | El punto principal es… |\n| **In short,…** | En resumen… |\n| **In other words,…** | En otras palabras… |\n| **What this means is…** | Lo que esto significa es… |\n| **The key detail is…** | El detalle clave es… |\n| **To sum up,…** | Para resumir… |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Team lead: The meeting has moved from Tuesday to Thursday because two people are unavailable. Please send your update by Wednesday afternoon so we can review it before the meeting.\n> Colleague: In short, the meeting is now on Thursday, and my update is due Wednesday afternoon.\n> Team lead: Exactly.\n> Colleague: I'll send it by three o'clock on Wednesday.

**Questions**
1. What day was the meeting originally planned for?\n2. What is the new meeting day?\n3. When is the update due?\n4. Why was the meeting moved?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Listen to a short announcement, then summarise it in one or two sentences without copying it word for word. Include the main point and two key details. If alone, use the script above as a listening exercise with text-to-speech; hide the text until after listening.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- State the main point accurately.\n- Include two key details.\n- Use your own wording rather than copying full sentences.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
