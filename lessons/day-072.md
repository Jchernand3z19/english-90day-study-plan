# Day 072 — Understanding expressions from context

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use surrounding clues to infer the meaning of an unfamiliar expression without translating every word.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| Expression | Meaning / clue |\n|---|---|\n| **I'm running out of time.** | Me queda poco tiempo; the person has a deadline. |\n| **That rings a bell.** | Me suena; the person recognises something vaguely. |\n| **It's up to you.** | Tú decides; the choice belongs to you. |\n| **I'm tied up right now.** | Estoy ocupado; the person cannot talk now. |\n| **That was a piece of cake.** | Fue muy fácil; the task was easy. |\n| **Let's call it a day.** | Dejémoslo por hoy; stop working for now. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples and explain their meaning in Spanish.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Bea: Can you help me with this form?\n> Max: I'd like to, but I'm tied up right now. I have a deadline in ten minutes.\n> Bea: No problem. Can we do it later?\n> Max: Sure. Let's call it a day at five and finish it together tomorrow.\n> Bea: Thanks. That works for me.

**Questions**
1. Why can't Max help immediately?\n2. What clue tells you what “tied up” means?\n3. When do they plan to finish the form?\n4. What does “call it a day” mean in this context?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

For each expression, invent a short context that makes its meaning clear. Then listen to a partner or AI tutor use one expression in a sentence and explain what clue helped you understand it. Do not guess from a literal word-for-word translation.

Use keywords, not a complete script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Explain at least four expressions in Spanish.\n- Identify one context clue for two expressions.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. If you still need a full script, mark **Review** and repeat the task with fewer notes.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target.
