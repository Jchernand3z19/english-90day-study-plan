# Day 072 — Understanding expressions from context

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** use surrounding clues to infer the meaning of an unfamiliar expression without translating every word.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| Expression | Meaning / clue |
|---|---|
| **I'm running out of time.** | Me queda poco tiempo; the person has a deadline. |
| **That rings a bell.** | Me suena; the person recognises something vaguely. |
| **It's up to you.** | Tú decides; the choice belongs to you. |
| **I'm tied up right now.** | Estoy ocupado; the person cannot talk now. |
| **That was a piece of cake.** | Fue muy fácil; the task was easy. |
| **Let's call it a day.** | Dejémoslo por hoy; stop working for now. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples and explain their meaning in Spanish.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Bea: Can you help me with this form?
> Max: I'd like to, but I'm tied up right now. I have a deadline in ten minutes.
> Bea: No problem. Can we do it later?
> Max: Sure. Let's call it a day and finish it together tomorrow.
> Bea: Thanks. That works for me.

**Questions**
1. Why can't Max help immediately?
2. What clue tells you what “tied up” means?
3. When do they plan to finish the form?
4. What does “call it a day” mean in this context?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

For each expression, invent a short context that makes its meaning clear. Then listen to a partner or AI tutor use one expression in a sentence and explain what clue helped you understand it. Do not guess from a literal word-for-word translation.

Use keywords, not a complete script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Explain at least four expressions in Spanish.
- Identify one context clue for two expressions.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. If you still need a full script, mark **Review** and repeat the task with fewer notes.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target.
