# Day 031 — Planificar un viaje

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** plan a short trip by asking about the destination, travel time, ticket cost, departure time, and one booking step.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 3–5 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Plan a trip: useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Where should we go?** | ¿Adónde deberíamos ir? |
| **How long does it take?** | ¿Cuánto se tarda? |
| **How much is a return ticket?** | ¿Cuánto cuesta un boleto de ida y vuelta? |
| **What time does it leave?** | ¿A qué hora sale? |
| **We should book in advance.** | Deberíamos reservar con anticipación. |
| **I'd rather travel by…** | Preferiría viajar en… |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and retrieve its meaning. Then plan a fictional weekend trip using a destination, transport, approximate cost, departure time, and one booking action. Clearly label fictional details as role-play.


## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this travel-planning conversation twice without showing you the script first.

> Dani: I'd like to plan a weekend trip. Where should we go?
>
> Alex: We could visit the coast. It takes about two hours by bus.
>
> Dani: How much is the ticket?
>
> Alex: Around fifteen dollars each way.
>
> Dani: Great. What time does the first bus leave?
>
> Alex: At eight in the morning. We should book the tickets in advance.

**Questions**
1. Where do they want to go?
2. How long does the bus journey take?
3. How much is one ticket each way?
4. What should they do in advance?

Answer before checking the script. Then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Production — 15 minutes

**Main task: plan a weekend trip.** Speak for about 30–45 seconds using the model and keywords. A longer plan is optional only if this is comfortable. Include:
1. Destination and one reason for choosing it.
2. Transport and journey time.
3. Ticket price and departure time.
4. One thing to book in advance.

Then answer a tutor's follow-up question about cost or timing. Ask one clarification question yourself. Repeat the plan with a different destination or transport option.


## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Without looking at your notes:
- Say three useful phrases for today's topic.
- Use at least two phrases in original sentences or a dialogue.
- Explain the meaning of your examples.
- Ask one follow-up question or clarification question.

**Suggested pass:** at least 80% of the check is completed correctly and the main task is understandable. For speaking-focused days, attempting the oral task is required. If you need to read every answer, mark **Revisión** rather than **Aprobado**.
