# Day 039 — Contar qué hiciste ayer

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** describe yesterday using past-simple verbs and simple sequence words.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 3–5 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Yesterday, I…** | Ayer yo… |
| **I went to…** | Fui a… |
| **I had lunch at…** | Almorcé en… |
| **After that,…** | Después de eso… |
| **Then I…** | Luego yo… |
| **I got home at…** | Llegué a casa a las… |
| **It was… because…** | Fue… porque… |

Say each phrase aloud. Cover the Spanish meaning and try to retrieve it. Complete one model sentence with your own or fictional detail.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Kim: What did you do yesterday?
> Pat: I went to work in the morning. After that, I had lunch with a colleague.
> Kim: What did you do in the afternoon?
> Pat: I finished a report, then I went to the supermarket.
> Kim: What time did you get home?
> Pat: I got home at six, and I cooked dinner.

**Questions**
1. Where did Pat go in the morning?
2. Who did Pat have lunch with?
3. What did Pat do after work?
4. What time did Pat get home?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Say two or three short sentences about yesterday using “I went…”, “I had…” or “After that…”. Use one or two past actions first. Add a follow-up answer only if comfortable.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish, then repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use at least four past-tense verbs.
- Use **then**, **after that**, or **finally**.
- Answer a follow-up question.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and understandable oral production. If the core task still requires reading a full script, mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
