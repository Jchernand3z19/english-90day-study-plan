# Day 045 — Explicar un problema

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** describe a practical problem, explain its impact, and ask for a specific solution politely.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Record actual counts and choose up to five difficult phrases to use in new sentences.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **There's a problem with…** | Hay un problema con… |
| **It doesn't work.** | No funciona. |
| **I expected…, but…** | Esperaba…, pero… |
| **Could you check it, please?** | ¿Podría revisarlo, por favor? |
| **What can we do about it?** | ¿Qué podemos hacer al respecto? |
| **Is there another option?** | ¿Hay otra opción? |

Say each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Guest: Excuse me, there's a problem with my room. The air conditioning doesn't work.
> Receptionist: I'm sorry about that. When did you notice it?
> Guest: About an hour ago. Could you check it, please?
> Receptionist: Of course. I can send someone up, or we can change your room.
> Guest: Thank you. Could I move to another room if it can't be fixed?
> Receptionist: Certainly. I'll check what's available.

**Questions**
1. What is the problem?
2. When did the guest notice it?
3. What two options does the receptionist offer?
4. What does the guest ask for if the problem cannot be fixed?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Explain a real or fictional service problem in three parts: what happened, why it matters, and what solution you want. Role-play with a staff member and respond to one follow-up question.

Use keywords rather than a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Describe the problem clearly.
- Ask for a specific action or solution.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review** and practise the weak skill again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
