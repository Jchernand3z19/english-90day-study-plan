# Day 022 — Daily routines and time

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** describe a daily routine and ask about the time someone does common activities.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| What time do you get up? | ¿A qué hora te levantas? / ¿A qué hora se levanta usted? |
| I usually get up at six thirty. | Normalmente me levanto a las seis y media. |
| I have breakfast. | Desayuno. |
| I start work at eight. | Empiezo a trabajar a las ocho. |
| I finish at five. | Termino a las cinco. |
| Before work / after work | Antes del trabajo / después del trabajo |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and use them in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Ana: What time do you usually get up?  
Ben: I usually get up at six thirty.  
Ana: Do you have breakfast at home?  
Ben: Yes, I have eggs and coffee before work.  
Ana: What time do you start work?  
Ben: I start at eight and finish at five.

**Questions**
1. What time does Ben get up?  
2. Does he have breakfast at home?  
3. What does he have for breakfast?  
4. What time does he start work?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Start with two model sentences: “I get up at…” and “I have breakfast at…”. Complete them with real or clearly fictional times. Add “I start work at…” only if it applies. Asking another person a question is optional extension practice.

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
