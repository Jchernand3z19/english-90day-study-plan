# Day 049 — Necesidades básicas durante un viaje

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask for essential help while travelling and confirm that you understood the response.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts; do not estimate progress.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **Where is the nearest restroom?** | ¿Dónde está el baño más cercano? |
| **I need some help, please.** | Necesito ayuda, por favor. |
| **Is there a pharmacy nearby?** | ¿Hay una farmacia cerca? |
| **Could you show me on the map?** | ¿Podría mostrarme en el mapa? |
| **How much does it cost?** | ¿Cuánto cuesta? |
| **Can I pay by card?** | ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta? |
| **I don't understand. Could you speak more slowly?** | No entiendo. ¿Podría hablar más despacio? |
| **Let me check that I understood.** | Déjeme comprobar que entendí. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it from memory. Then make three original sentences about your own life or label them clearly as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing you the script first. If studying alone, cover the script while listening.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Clerk: Hello. Can I help you?
> Traveller: Yes, please. Is there a pharmacy nearby?
> Clerk: Yes. Go straight for one block and turn left. It's next to the bank.
> Traveller: Could you show me on the map, please?
> Clerk: Of course. It's about a five-minute walk.
> Traveller: Thank you. Can I pay by card at the pharmacy?
> Clerk: Most pharmacies accept cards, but you should check before paying.

**Comprehension questions**
1. What place does the traveller need?
2. Which direction should the traveller go first?
3. What is next to the pharmacy?
4. What should the traveller check before paying?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then shadow two lines and repeat them with natural rhythm. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening as **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Complete two role-plays: (A) ask for a nearby service and repeat the directions in your own words; (B) ask about price and payment. Include one clarification phrase and confirm one important detail.

Use keywords, not a full script. If using an AI tutor, ask it to play the other speaker, ask one follow-up question at a time, and explain corrections in Spanish. Repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask for a service politely.
- Understand or confirm simple directions.
- Ask about price or payment.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt when listening is assessed and an understandable oral response. If you cannot perform the core task without reading a complete script, mark **Review** and practise again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
