# Day 087 — Guided free conversation

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** sustain a conversation on a familiar topic while managing turns, asking follow-up questions, explaining opinions, and repairing misunderstandings.

## 1. Warm-up — 5 minutes

Review only five phrases you often forget. Say each phrase aloud before looking at the answer. Do not spend the entire session memorising lists.

## 2. Conversation toolkit — 10 minutes

| Purpose | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Start a topic | **What have you been interested in lately?** | ¿Qué le ha interesado últimamente? |
| Add detail | **One thing I noticed was…** | Algo que noté fue… |
| Give an opinion | **I think… because…** | Creo que… porque… |
| Follow up | **What happened after that?** | ¿Qué pasó después? |
| Clarify | **Could you explain that another way?** | ¿Podría explicarlo de otra manera? |
| Rephrase | **What I mean is…** | Lo que quiero decir es… |
| Close | **It was good talking with you.** | Fue un gusto conversar. |

## 3. Listening — 10–15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this conversation twice without showing it first.

> Nina: I've started learning to cook new dishes.  
> Omar: That's interesting. What made you start?  
> Nina: I wanted to eat at home more often and learn something useful.  
> Omar: What has been the hardest part?  
> Nina: Planning the time. Some recipes take longer than I expect.  
> Omar: That makes sense. Have you found a way to manage it?  
> Nina: Yes, I prepare some ingredients the night before.

Questions:
1. Why did Nina start learning to cook?
2. What has been the hardest part?
3. What solution has she found?
4. Which question does Omar ask to continue the conversation?

If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Free conversation — 15–20 minutes

Choose one topic: learning a skill, work, travel, food, exercise, or a recent experience. Have a five-minute conversation with a partner or AI tutor. Include:
- an opening question;
- at least three follow-up questions;
- one opinion with a reason;
- one clarification or rephrasing phrase if needed;
- a natural closing.

If practising alone, record a two-sided role-play. Do not label solo practice as a live conversation. Ask for feedback in Spanish after finishing, then repeat the weakest part.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Did you ask questions based on the other person's answers?
- Did you give details rather than only short answers?
- Did you explain an opinion or experience?
- Did you use a repair phrase if needed?
- Did you answer at least three listening questions correctly?

Record evidence and choose one skill to review before Day 89. Do not treat completion of this exercise as proof of fluency or a CEFR level.
