# Day 063 — Estar de acuerdo y discrepar con respeto

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** agree or disagree politely, acknowledge another viewpoint, and explain your own position.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **I agree with you.** | Estoy de acuerdo contigo. |
| **I see your point, but…** | Entiendo tu punto, pero… |
| **That's true, although…** | Es verdad, aunque… |
| **I understand what you mean.** | Entiendo lo que quieres decir. |
| **I'm not sure I agree because…** | No estoy seguro de estar de acuerdo porque… |
| **We may see it differently.** | Puede que lo veamos de manera diferente. |
| **That's a fair point.** | Es un punto válido. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it from memory. Then make three original sentences about your own life or label them clearly as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing you the script first.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Ana: I think people should study English for an hour every day.
> Ben: I see your point, but an hour can be difficult for busy people.
> Ana: That's true, although regular practice is important.
> Ben: I agree with that. Maybe twenty minutes a day is easier to maintain.
> Ana: That's a fair point. The best plan may depend on the person.

**Comprehension questions**
1. What does Ana suggest?
2. Why does Ben disagree with an hour every day?
3. What does Ben suggest instead?
4. What do they say the best plan depends on?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then shadow two lines and repeat them with natural rhythm. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening as **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Respond respectfully to this opinion: “Everyone should study English for two hours a day.” Acknowledge one reasonable point, then explain your view and give a reason. Finally, switch positions and try to defend the opposite view for 30 seconds.

Use keywords, not a full script. Ask for feedback in Spanish after your response, then repeat one corrected answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use one phrase that acknowledges the other viewpoint.
- State agreement or disagreement politely.
- Give a reason for your position.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt when assessed and an understandable oral response. If the core task is not yet possible without reading a complete script, mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
