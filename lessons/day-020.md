# Day 020 — Transport and getting around

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask about a destination, fare, departure time, and an alternative transport option.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from the current or previous lessons. Try to say each phrase aloud before looking at the notes, then use two phrases in a new answer. Record phrases recalled without help. Anki is not required.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| Does this bus go to the city centre? | ¿Este autobús va al centro? |
| Where can I buy a ticket? | ¿Dónde puedo comprar un boleto? |
| How much is a ticket? | ¿Cuánto cuesta un boleto? |
| What time does it leave? | ¿A qué hora sale? |
| Where should I get off? | ¿Dónde debo bajarme? |
| Can I take a bus instead? | ¿Puedo tomar un autobús en su lugar? |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and recall the meaning. Choose three phrases and use them in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Visitor: Excuse me, how do I get to the museum?  
Local: Go straight and turn right at the bank.  
Visitor: Is it far?  
Local: No, it's about ten minutes on foot.  
Visitor: Can I take a bus instead?  
Local: Yes. Take bus number five and get off at the museum stop.  
Visitor: How much is a ticket, and what time does the next bus leave?  
Local: A ticket costs two dollars. The next bus leaves in fifteen minutes.

**Questions**
1. Where does the visitor want to go?  
2. Where should the visitor turn right?  
3. How long is the walk?  
4. Which bus can the visitor take?  
5. How much does a ticket cost?  
6. When does the next bus leave?

Answer aloud before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Ask for transport to a destination. Include the route, ticket price or departure time, and one follow-up question.

If a tutor is available, ask for one or two corrections in Spanish, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
