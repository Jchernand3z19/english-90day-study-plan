# Day 013 — Shopping role-play: size, fitting room, and payment

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** combine price questions, simple purchases, and polite requests in a short everyday role-play.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose only 2–3 familiar phrases. Try each before looking, then use one in a modelled answer. Anki is optional.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| How much is this? | ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? |
| Do you have this in medium? | ¿Lo tiene en talla mediana? |
| I'd like to try this on, please. | Me gustaría probármelo, por favor. |
| Where are the fitting rooms, please? | ¿Dónde están los probadores, por favor? |
| Can I pay by card? | ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta? |
| Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and try to retrieve the meaning. Choose one phrase and practise it with a different size or item.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Customer: Excuse me, how much is this shirt?
Clerk: It's twelve dollars.
Customer: Do you have it in medium?
Clerk: Yes, we do. Would you like to try it on?
Customer: Yes, please. Where are the fitting rooms?
Clerk: They're at the back, next to the mirrors.
Customer: It fits well. I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Clerk: Yes, of course.

**Questions**
1. How much is the shirt?
2. Which size does the customer ask for?
3. Where are the fitting rooms?
4. How does the customer want to pay?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

This is a review of Days 8–12, not a new test. Practise just three steps with the model visible: ask the price; ask for a size; say “I'll take it.” Add the fitting-room or payment question only if the first three steps are comfortable. Switching roles is optional.

Ask for one or two corrections in Spanish if a tutor is available, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
