# Day 013 — Review shopping and everyday requests

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** combine price questions, simple purchases, and polite requests in a short everyday role-play.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it and record only the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| How much is this? | ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? |
| Do you have this in medium? | ¿Lo tiene en talla mediana? |
| I'd like a sandwich, please. | Quisiera un sándwich, por favor. |
| Could I have the menu, please? | ¿Me trae el menú, por favor? |
| Could I have this without onions? | ¿Podría prepararlo sin cebolla? |
| Could you help me, please? | ¿Podría ayudarme, por favor? |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and try to retrieve the meaning. Choose three phrases to use in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Customer: Excuse me, how much is this shirt?  
Clerk: It's twelve dollars.  
Customer: Great. I'll take it. Can I pay by card?  
Clerk: Yes, of course.  
Customer: Thank you. Also, could I have a table for two at the restaurant next door?  
Host: Certainly. Do you have a reservation?  
Customer: No. Is a table available now?

**Questions**
1. How much is the shirt?  
2. How does the customer want to pay?  
3. How many people need a table?  
4. Does the customer have a reservation?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Choose shopping or a restaurant. Ask two questions and make one polite request. Try to complete the exchange without reading the full script.

Ask for one or two corrections in Spanish if a tutor is available, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
