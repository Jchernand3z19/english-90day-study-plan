# Day 010 — Ordering food and drinks

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** order a simple meal, ask about an item, and understand a basic price.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each answer before revealing it and record only the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| I'd like…, please. | Quisiera…, por favor. |
| Could I have…? | ¿Me podría dar…? |
| Would you like fries with that? | ¿Quiere papas fritas con eso? |
| No, thank you. | No, gracias. |
| How much is the sandwich? | ¿Cuánto cuesta el sándwich? |
| That's all, thank you. | Eso es todo, gracias. |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish, and try to retrieve the meaning. Choose three phrases to use in original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — check only after listening**

Server: Hello. Are you ready to order?  
Customer: Yes, I'd like a chicken sandwich and a glass of water, please.  
Server: Would you like fries with that?  
Customer: No, thank you. How much is the sandwich?  
Server: It's eight dollars.  
Customer: Great. That's all, thank you.

**Questions**
1. What food does the customer order?  
2. What drink does the customer ask for?  
3. Does the customer want fries?  
4. How much is the sandwich?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**; reading is not listening evidence.

## 4. Speaking practice — 15 minutes

Order one main item and a drink. Respond to one follow-up question and ask about the price. Practise once as the server and once as the customer.

Ask for one or two corrections in Spanish if a tutor is available, repeat the corrected sentence, and try once more with less help.
