# Day 082 — Connectors for developing ideas

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** connect related ideas clearly so a spoken explanation is easier to follow.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it and record the actual number reviewed.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| Connector | Español | Example |\n|---|---|---|\n| **First,…** | Primero… | First, I check the information. |\n| **Also,…** | Además… | Also, I ask if anything is missing. |\n| **However,…** | Sin embargo… | However, the process can take time. |\n| **As a result,…** | Como resultado… | As a result, we avoid repeating the work. |\n| **For example,…** | Por ejemplo… | For example, I use a checklist. |\n| **Finally,…** | Finalmente… | Finally, I share the results. |

Say each expression aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it. Make two original examples.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Priya: How do you organise a busy day?\n> Mateo: First, I write down my priorities. Also, I check which tasks have deadlines.\n> Priya: Does that always work?\n> Mateo: Not always. However, it helps me see what is urgent. As a result, I can plan my time better.\n> Priya: And how do you finish?\n> Mateo: Finally, I review what is still pending.

**Questions**
1. What does Mateo do first?\n2. What else does he check?\n3. What limitation does he mention?\n4. What is the result of planning?

Answer before checking the script, then shadow two lines. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Explain how you complete a familiar task in 60–90 seconds. Use at least four connectors to show sequence, addition, contrast, result, or conclusion. Then repeat the explanation with a different connector in one place.

Use keywords, not a full script. Request feedback in Spanish and repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Use at least four connectors correctly.\n- Keep the explanation in a logical order.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt and an understandable oral response. Otherwise mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
