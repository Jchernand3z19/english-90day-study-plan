## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the exchange twice without showing it first. Listen for the letters in the surname, then answer the questions before checking the text.

A: Could you spell your last name, please?  
B: Sure. H-E-R-N-A-N-D-E-Z.  
A: Sorry, could you say that again?  
B: Of course. H-E-R-N-A-N-D-E-Z.  
A: Thank you.  
B: You're welcome.

**Questions**
1. What does Speaker A ask Speaker B to do?
2. Which phrase does Speaker A use to request repetition?
3. How does Speaker A respond at the end?

Use a fictional name if you prefer. Repeat the exchange three times. If you did not actually hear it, mark listening **Not assessed**.
