# Day 004 — Spell a name and ask for repetition

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 40–50 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** recognise a few letter names, spell a short example, and use one repair phrase.

## 1. Recall — 5 minutes

Repeat “What's your name?” and “My name is…”.

## 2. Learn the useful language — 10–15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| How do you spell your name? | ¿Cómo se deletrea tu nombre? |
| A, B, C… | A, B, C… |
| Could you repeat that, please? | ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor? |
| Slowly, please. | Despacio, por favor. |
| Thank you. | Gracias. |

Focus on hearing and repeating the letters in **SAM** and **ALEX**. Do not memorise the entire alphabet in one session.

## 3. Guided listening — 10 minutes

Listen twice without reading:

> A: How do you spell your name?  
> B: S-A-M.  
> A: Could you repeat that, please?  
> B: S-A-M.  
> A: Thank you.

Answer:
1. Which name is spelled?
2. How many letters do you hear?
3. Which phrase asks for repetition?

If audio was not played, mark listening **Not assessed**. Reveal the script after listening.

## 4. Supported speaking — 10–15 minutes

Spell **SAM** slowly, one letter at a time. Then spell **ALEX** if comfortable. Practise saying “Could you repeat that, please?” twice. Reading the phrase is allowed.

## 5. Check — 5 minutes

Can you spell SAM and ask someone to repeat a word? Record what was easy and what needs more practice. Mark *Review* if letter names are still unclear.

**Completion rule:** this lesson does not require spelling your surname or producing a long conversation.
