# Day 004 — Personal Details, Numbers, and Spelling

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** share simple personal details and ask someone to spell or repeat information.

## 1. Anki warm-up — 10 minutes

Review phrases from Days 1–3. Choose five and say them aloud before revealing the answers.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| How do you spell your name? | ¿Cómo se escribe tu nombre? |
| Could you spell that, please? | ¿Podrías deletrearlo, por favor? |
| Could you say that again? | ¿Podrías decirlo otra vez? |
| My email address is… | Mi correo electrónico es… |
| My phone number is… | Mi número de teléfono es… |
| I didn't catch that. | No alcancé a entender eso. |
| Let me check. | Déjame revisar. |
| It's spelled… | Se escribe… |
| The number is… | El número es… |

### Practice spelling

Say the letters of your first name slowly in English. Then practice a fictional email address or phone number; do not put private contact details in the repository.

Useful number examples:

- 13: thirteen
- 30: thirty
- 14: fourteen
- 40: forty
- 15: fifteen
- 50: fifty

Listen for the difference between -teen and -ty. Stress often helps distinguish the two.

## 3. Listen and shadow — 15 minutes

Practice this exchange:

A: Could you spell your last name, please?  
B: Sure. H-E-R-N-A-N-D-E-Z.  
A: Sorry, could you say that again?  
B: Of course. H-E-R-N-A-N-D-E-Z.  
A: Thank you.  
B: You're welcome.

Use a fictional name if you prefer. Repeat the exchange three times.

## 4. Speak — 15 minutes

1. Spell your first name aloud.
2. Ask someone to spell a name.
3. Ask for a repetition politely.
4. Say a fictional email address slowly.
5. Read five numbers aloud, then repeat them from memory.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

Produce the English for:

1. ¿Cómo se escribe tu nombre?
2. ¿Podrías repetirlo, por favor?
3. No alcancé a entender eso.
4. Déjame revisar.
5. Se escribe…

**Suggested pass:** at least 4 of 5 phrases are understandable, and you can spell a name or ask for clarification without reading the full dialogue.

## Evidence

Log the number phrases recalled, whether you practiced spelling, the self-check score, and any pronunciation difficulties. Do not record private phone numbers, email addresses, or other sensitive personal data in the repository.
