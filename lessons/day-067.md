# Day 067 — Repaso de la semana 9

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 25–35 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** review opinions, reasons, respectful disagreement, and short debate skills, then choose the next step based on demonstrated performance.

This is a lighter review day. Do not add new language just to fill time.

## 1. Retrieval — 5 minutes

Without notes, say one phrase for each purpose:
- give an opinion;
- explain a reason;
- give an example;
- disagree politely;
- summarise a discussion.

Then check your notes and mark which phrases you recalled independently.

## 2. Short listening — 10 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read this twice without showing you the script first.

> Alex: I think online learning is convenient because I can study at home.  
> Bea: I agree that it's convenient, but I sometimes miss talking to other students.  
> Alex: That's a fair point. Maybe a mix of online and in-person learning would work well.

Questions:
1. What does Alex like about online learning?
2. What does Bea miss?
3. What alternative do they suggest?

If you did not hear the text, mark listening **Not assessed**.

## 3. Speaking review — 10–15 minutes

Answer aloud: **“Is it better to learn alone or with other people?”** Speak for 60 seconds, give one reason and one example, then respond respectfully to the opposite opinion.

Review the recordings or notes from Days 61–66 if available. If not, use today's attempt as a new baseline; do not invent a comparison.

## 4. Mastery decision — 5 minutes

- **Ready:** you can state an opinion, give a reason/example, and respond respectfully without reading a full script.
- **Review:** one or more of these skills is still difficult; repeat the weakest task and record the correction.
- **Not assessed:** no oral attempt or no listening evidence when listening is required.

Record one strength and one next target. This review day does not automatically approve the week or phase.
