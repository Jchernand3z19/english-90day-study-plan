# Day 007 — Week 1 review and recovery

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** review the first six days without adding new language or demanding a long speech.

## 1. Quick recall — 5 minutes

Try these four chunks. Looking at the notes is allowed after your first attempt:
- Hi.
- My name is…
- I'm from…
- I like…

## 2. Match the meaning — 5–8 minutes

Say or identify the English phrase for each meaning:

1. Hola.
2. Me llamo…
3. Soy de Honduras.
4. Me gusta la música.
5. ¿Cómo te llamas?
6. ¿Podría repetir eso, por favor?

Use the lesson pages to check your answers. This is practice, not a scored exam.

## 3. Short supported exchange — 5–10 minutes

Use the model:
- A: Hi. What's your name?
- B: My name is ______.
- A: Where are you from?
- B: I'm from ______.
- A: What do you like?
- B: I like ______.

Read it once. Then try to say only your three answers without reading. If that is difficult, repeat the model; do not force a longer speech.

## 4. Reflection — 3–5 minutes

Record:
- Two phrases you can say comfortably.
- One phrase that still needs help.
- Whether you heard the listening audio in the lessons (do not count reading scripts as listening).

## Review decision

- **Ready to continue:** you can communicate your name and country with understandable versions of the models.
- **Needs review:** repeat the specific sentence frames that remain difficult.
- **Rest:** if you did not practise, leave the session pending.

No new grammar topic or 45–60 second introduction is required. Do not approve the day without actual practice evidence.
