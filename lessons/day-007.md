# Day 007 — Week 1 Review and Recovery

**Phase:** 1 — Foundations and daily life  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Goal:** consolidate the first six days without adding a large amount of new material.

This is a light review day. If you are tired, take a rest and continue when ready. A rest day does not count as an approved lesson unless the planned review is actually completed.

## 1. Anki review — 10 minutes

Review due cards. Before revealing each answer, try to say the phrase aloud. Record phrases recalled and phrases that need more practice.

## 2. Recall without looking — 5 minutes

Try to produce these meanings in English:

1. Hola, ¿cómo estás?
2. Me llamo…
3. ¿De dónde eres?
4. ¿A qué te dedicas?
5. ¿Cómo se escribe tu nombre?
6. ¿Podrías repetirlo, por favor?
7. ¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre?
8. Eso suena interesante.
9. ¿Y tú?
10. Que tengas un buen día.

Do not check the answers until you have tried all ten.

## 3. Speaking challenge — 5–10 minutes

Give a 45–60 second introduction. Include your name, where you are from, what you do, one interest, and why you are learning English.

Then answer three questions as if meeting someone new. Ask one question back to keep the exchange going.

## 4. Reflection

Record:

- Which phrases were easiest to recall?
- Which three phrases still need practice?
- Was it easier to read than to speak without looking?
- What should be reviewed before starting Day 8?

## Review decision

- **Ready to continue:** most target phrases are understandable and you can complete a short introduction.
- **Needs review:** several basic phrases remain difficult; repeat the relevant lesson before advancing.
- **Rest:** if you did not study today, leave this session pending and do not claim it as completed.

No new grammar topic is required today. The goal is to make familiar phrases easier to retrieve.
