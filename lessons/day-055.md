# Day 055 — Hacer preguntas de seguimiento

**Phase:** 2 — Travel, socializing, and stories  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** ask relevant follow-up questions that help a conversation continue naturally.

## 1. Phrase retrieval — 10 minutes

Choose 5–10 useful phrases from this lesson or a previous one. Try to say each before looking, then use two in new answers. Record what you recalled without help; no deck is required for this step.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |
|---|---|
| **What was that like?** | ¿Cómo fue eso? |
| **What happened next?** | ¿Qué pasó después? |
| **Why did you choose that?** | ¿Por qué eligió eso? |
| **How did you feel about it?** | ¿Cómo se sintió al respecto? |
| **What do you like most about it?** | ¿Qué es lo que más le gusta? |
| **Could you tell me a little more?** | ¿Podría contarme un poco más? |
| **Really? Then what happened?** | ¿En serio? ¿Y qué pasó después? |
| **Would you do it again?** | ¿Lo volvería a hacer? |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it from memory. Then make three original sentences about your own life or label them clearly as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing you the script first. If studying alone, cover the script while listening.

****Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Ana: I tried a cooking class last weekend.
> Rob: Really? What was that like?
> Ana: It was fun, but the recipe was difficult.
> Rob: What was the hardest part?
> Ana: Making the sauce. I had to try twice.
> Rob: Would you do it again?
> Ana: Yes. I'd like to learn another recipe.

**Comprehension questions**
1. What did Ana do last weekend?
2. What was difficult?
3. How many times did she try to make the sauce?
4. Would she take another class?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then shadow two lines and repeat them with natural rhythm. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening as **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Answer this prompt: “I went somewhere new last weekend.” Ask five follow-up questions, one at a time, and respond to the answers. Then switch roles: answer five questions about a real or clearly labelled fictional experience. Avoid asking five unrelated questions in a row; make each question connect to the previous answer.

Use keywords, not a full script. If using an AI tutor, ask it to play the other speaker, ask one follow-up question at a time, and explain corrections in Spanish. Repeat one corrected response.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Ask at least five relevant follow-up questions.
- Ask one question based on a detail the other person gave.
- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.
- Keep the exchange going for at least two minutes.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt when listening is assessed and an understandable oral response. If you cannot perform the core task without reading a complete script, mark **Review** and practise again.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, phrases recalled without help, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
