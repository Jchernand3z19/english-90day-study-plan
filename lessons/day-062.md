# Day 062 — Dar razones y ejemplos

**Phase:** 3 — Fluency, discussion, and nuance  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Status:** Pending  
**Objective:** support an opinion with a reason and a specific example.

## 1. Anki and retrieval — 10 minutes

Review due cards from your 1,000 common English phrases deck. Say each phrase before revealing it. Choose up to five difficult phrases and use them in new sentences. Record actual counts; do not estimate progress.

## 2. Useful language — 15 minutes

| English | Español |\n|---|---|\n| **One reason is…** | Una razón es… |\n| **For example,…** | Por ejemplo… |\n| **For instance,…** | Por ejemplo… |\n| **This is important because…** | Esto es importante porque… |\n| **A good example is…** | Un buen ejemplo es… |\n| **That's why…** | Por eso… |\n| **The main reason is…** | La razón principal es… |

Read each phrase aloud, cover the Spanish meaning, and retrieve it from memory. Then make three original sentences about your own life or label them clearly as role-play.

## 3. Listening and shadowing — 15 minutes

Ask a tutor or text-to-speech tool to read the dialogue below twice without showing you the script first.

**Listening script — reveal only after two listens**

> Mia: I think walking is a good way to stay active.\n> Tom: Why do you think so?\n> Mia: One reason is that you don't need special equipment. For example, I walk for twenty minutes after work.\n> Tom: That's true. It can also help you relax.\n> Mia: Exactly. That's why I try to walk most days.

**Comprehension questions**
1. What does Mia think is a good way to stay active?\n2. What is her first reason?\n3. Give the example she mentions.\n4. What additional benefit does Tom mention?

Answer aloud before checking the script. Then shadow two lines and repeat them with natural rhythm. If you did not actually hear the dialogue, mark listening as **Not assessed**.

## 4. Speaking production — 15 minutes

Choose a familiar topic and speak for 60 seconds using this structure: opinion → reason → example → short conclusion. Topics: learning English, walking, cooking at home, or using public transport. Then ask a partner to ask “Why?” and answer with a specific example.

Use keywords, not a full script. Ask for feedback in Spanish after your response, then repeat one corrected answer.

## 5. Self-check — 5 minutes

- Give one clear opinion and one reason.\n- Add a specific example, not only a general statement.\n- Use **because**, **for example**, or **that's why**.\n- Answer at least three listening questions correctly.

**Suggested pass:** at least 8/10 on the daily rubric, with an actual listening attempt when assessed and an understandable oral response. If the core task is not yet possible without reading a complete script, mark **Review**.

## Evidence and completion

Record the date, actual time spent, Anki cards reviewed, listening method, speaking task, score, corrections, and next review target in your daily log. Keep the lesson **Pending** until started; use **In progress** while working, **Review** if more practice is needed, and **Approved** only when evidence meets the rubric.
