# Study Method and Rules

## Purpose

This repository is a structured 90-day practice plan for practical conversation. The 90 days are a guide, not a deadline. Progress depends on demonstrated ability, not on the calendar.

## Standard study cycle

1. **Retrieve:** recall one to three familiar phrases; do not begin with a large phrase list.
2. **Understand:** learn a small number of new chunks, with Spanish meaning and a clear model.
3. **Listen:** hear the exact dialogue or audio assigned to the lesson without reading first.
4. **Imitate:** repeat one short line at a time, first slowly and then naturally.
5. **Build:** complete a sentence frame by changing one word or personal detail.
6. **Produce:** answer one question or perform a short supported exchange. Remove the model only when the learner is ready.
7. **Correct and retry:** give one or two corrections in Spanish, then repeat the corrected phrase.
8. **Review later:** retrieve the same useful phrase in a later session.

## Language balance: Spanish + English

- At the beginning, explain new grammar, confusing expressions, and corrections in Spanish.
- Practise useful phrases in English immediately after understanding the meaning.
- Start with one-word or short phrase answers when needed; progress to a modelled sentence, a sentence with one substitution, a short exchange, and only later a longer answer.
- Never require an unsupported sentence, long introduction, or open-ended role-play before the relevant language has been taught and practised.
- Teach no more than a few new chunks at once in the first beginner lessons; retrieval volume must match what the learner has actually studied.
- Gradually reduce Spanish support only when the learner can complete the task comfortably.
- Do not force an all-English lesson before the learner can understand the task.
- Prioritise conversation and interaction over long grammar explanations or isolated vocabulary lists.

## Mastery gate: when to advance

The numbered day is a suggested sequence, not a requirement to move on. Use the following decision after each core lesson:

- **Ready to advance:** the learner can complete the main speaking task with understandable English, retrieve the target phrases with little or no help, and meet the stated check (normally at least 8/10).
- **Needs review:** the main message is unclear, target phrases cannot be recalled or used, or the learner needs to read a complete script. Repeat the core task with simpler prompts and revisit it later.
- **Incomplete / not assessed:** the speaking task or required listening task was not actually attempted. Do not mark the lesson approved.
- **Rest/review day:** use it to revisit the weakest skill; it does not automatically approve a lesson or phase.

Do not require perfect grammar or a native accent. The essential standard is understandable communication and evidence that the target skill can be used, not memorised recitation. If a core prerequisite is not secure, delay the next difficult topic and reinforce the prerequisite first.

## Standard 60-minute session

Use the times in each lesson as guidance. A typical session may include retrieval, target language, listening, speaking, and a short self-check. Review/rest lessons should be shorter and their time allocations should match the stated total.

## How to use an AI tutor

The tutor must follow the exact lesson file currently being studied. It must not invent a different dialogue, replace the assigned listening text, or introduce harder questions that are not part of the lesson. Ask one question at a time, wait for the learner's answer, and do not supply the answer prematurely. For listening, read or play the exact assigned dialogue twice without displaying it first; ask only the lesson's comprehension questions. If audio is unavailable, label listening **Not assessed** and continue with a separate speaking activity. Do not call an improvised exercise a completed lesson or a valid diagnostic.

Suggested prompt:

> Act as my patient English conversation tutor. My current level is approximately A1/A2. Prioritise real conversation. Explain difficult points and corrections in Spanish, then help me practise in English. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Do not give me a complete answer before I try. Start with short answers and gradually ask for more detail. If I cannot use a target phrase independently, simplify the prompt and practise it again. If my answer is ambiguous or may reflect a misunderstanding of the question, ask one brief clarification before correcting it or recording an error; do not infer personal details from unclear wording. Assess the actual response, give one or two corrections, ask me to retry, and do not claim I passed without evidence.

For role-play, tell the tutor the situation and ask it to stay in character. Request feedback after the exchange so the conversation can flow.

## Evidence and honesty

- Record only work that actually happened.
- Distinguish listening to audio from reading text aloud.
- Do not claim a recording exists unless one was made.
- Do not count a phrase as mastered because it was seen once.
- Do not mark a session approved without a completed task and evaluation.
- If a session is missed, keep it pending and resume it later; do not automatically skip it or mark it passed.
- If listening cannot be performed, mark it **Not assessed** rather than inventing a score.

## Phrase retrieval — Anki is optional support

The original 1,000-phrase deck was inspected and a separate ordered copy was prepared. The learner reports importing the ordered copy and says the initial check looks correct. The package audit is documented in [the Anki tracker](vocabulary/anki-tracker.md). This does not prove long-term workload suitability or phrase mastery.

Anki is optional and must support—not replace—the lesson's listening, speaking, and contextual phrase use. For a retrieval block:

1. Start with one to three phrases that have already been taught.
2. Try to say each phrase before looking at the answer; support is allowed.
3. Use one phrase in a short answer or modelled exchange.
4. Record phrases recalled without help and phrases needing review. Increase the amount only when recall is comfortable. If Anki is used, record the actual cards reviewed/new cards separately; do not equate a correct card response with spoken mastery.

The ordered copy is configured for 13 new cards per active study day, but this is a starting setting, not a requirement. After several sessions, reduce new cards if the review load becomes excessive. Do not automatically increase the new-card count to catch up after missed days. The package is grouped by broad phase and category, not mapped exactly to each lesson/day. Duplicate wording was retained where contexts or translations may differ; audio presence does not certify pronunciation quality or translation accuracy.

## Level expectations

A1/A2 is the estimated starting range, not a formal diagnosis. The goal is greater independence and conversational confidence. Reaching B1 or B2 in 90 days cannot be guaranteed; progress depends on starting ability, practice quality, listening exposure, and feedback. Use a recognised assessment if you need an official level.
