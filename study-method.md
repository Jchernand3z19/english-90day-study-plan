# Study Method and Rules

## Purpose

This repository is a structured 90-day practice plan. Its purpose is to build practical conversational ability through repeated retrieval, listening, shadowing, speaking, and honest feedback.

## Standard study cycle

1. **Retrieve:** attempt to remember familiar phrases before looking.
2. **Understand:** learn a small set of useful phrases in context.
3. **Listen:** hear natural pronunciation when audio is available.
4. **Imitate:** repeat short chunks, focusing on rhythm and stress.
5. **Produce:** answer a question or perform a role-play without reading a full script.
6. **Correct:** note a small number of meaningful errors.
7. **Repeat:** use the corrected phrase again later.

## Keep the work manageable

- Prioritize phrases that help you communicate in real situations.
- Learn complete expressions rather than isolated word lists only.
- Start with short answers, then add one detail, reason, or follow-up question.
- Use Spanish explanations when needed; gradually attempt more of the task in English.
- Review difficult material across several sessions instead of trying to master everything at once.
- Take rest days when needed. Do not mark a rest day as a completed lesson.

## How to use an AI tutor

Suggested prompt:

> Act as my patient English conversation tutor. My current level is approximately A1/A2. Explain difficult points in Spanish, but ask me one question at a time in English. Wait for my answer. Do not give me the full answer before I try. Correct the most important errors, explain briefly in Spanish, and ask me to try again. Use natural, common English. Do not claim I passed unless I complete the task and meet the stated criteria.

For role-play, tell the tutor the situation and ask it to stay in character. Request feedback after the exchange so the conversation can flow.

## Evidence and honesty

- Record only work that actually happened.
- Distinguish listening to audio from reading text aloud.
- Do not claim a recording exists unless one was made.
- Do not count a phrase as mastered because it was seen once.
- Do not mark a session approved without a completed task and evaluation.
- If a session is missed, keep it pending and resume it later; do not automatically skip it or mark it passed.

## Anki guidance

The target deck contains 1,000 common phrases. Use the deck's real statistics when updating counts. A phrase is considered mastered for this plan only when it can be understood, recalled without looking, used in a new sentence or dialogue, and retrieved again during a later review. This is a practical tracking rule, not a replacement for Anki's own scheduling algorithm.

## Level expectations

A1/A2 is the estimated starting range, not a formal diagnosis. The goal is greater independence and conversational confidence. Reaching B1 or B2 in 90 days cannot be guaranteed; progress depends on starting ability, practice quality, listening exposure, and feedback. Use a recognized assessment if you need an official level.
