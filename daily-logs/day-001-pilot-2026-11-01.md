## Pilot evaluation

**What worked**
1. One prompt at a time supported participation.
2. Short models and Spanish explanations helped the learner repair sentence forms.
3. The learner successfully reused corrected forms after modelling.
4. The conversation could respond to personal meaning rather than remain a list of memorised deck phrases.

**What needs correction**
1. The tutor must follow the exact lesson sequence and distinguish core tasks from optional extensions.
2. Do not call a text transcript a pronunciation assessment or a listening test.
3. After a correction, check understanding and later retrieval in a new prompt; repetition immediately after a model is supported practice, not independent mastery.
4. The tutor should not prematurely declare the day complete while required listening and final self-check evidence are missing.
5. Avoid introducing extra questions (age, park, dominoes, music) before completing the lesson's three core frames, listening task, supported speaking, and self-check. These can be optional conversation extensions afterwards.
